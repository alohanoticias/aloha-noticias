Black Clover Is Back After 5 Years: Season 2 Schedule and the Dub Situation

Episodes air on Saturdays on Crunchyroll, the first part has 13 and the Brazilian Portuguese dub has already started. Here is what we know today.

By Aloha TeamNovember 7, 2026, 19:14

ShareWhatsAppXFacebookTelegram

Anyone who followed Black Clover in 2021 needed patience: the last episode of the first series aired on March 30 of that year, and the return only came on October 3, 2026, a Saturday at noon Brasília time, on Crunchyroll. The new season has been airing for over a month, so it is worth gathering the answers that are scattered across several sites.

When does each episode come out?

One a week, on Saturdays. The schedule published by AdoroCinema and O Vício is the same: episode 1 aired on October 3 and the first part ends with episode 13 on December 26. Episode 6 is set for November 7, episode 7 for the 14th, and the weekly pace continues from there. Both sites warn that the dates may change, and the time was only given for the premiere (noon); for later episodes, the pages give no time. All dates and times here are for Brazil, so in other countries they may differ.

The second part is set for 2027. AdoroCinema lists dates through episode 26, on March 27, but O Vício and Ei Nerd treat that total as a leak or rumor, and O Vício itself says the episode count of the second batch could still change. So 26 is the most likely figure, not an official number.

Where does the story stand?

The season adapts the invasion of the Spade Kingdom, when Asta and the Magic Knights try to rescue the captains captured by the Dark Triad. AdoroCinema, Ei Nerd and O Vício describe the arc the same way. O Vício adds, in a separate article, that the first part should close this arc (chapters 261 to 331 of the manga) and the second, in 2027, would cover the final arc. This comes from a single source.

What about the dub?

Here the story changed over the month. On October 6, AdoroCinema said the episodes arrived subtitled, with the original Japanese audio, and that Crunchyroll was expected to release the dub in the following months, with no date. On October 24, according to O Vício, the Brazilian Portuguese dub began: the season's first episode, which is episode 171 of the series overall, is already available in Portuguese, and new dubbed episodes arrive every Saturday at noon.

The same article names Yan Gesteira as the voice of Asta and Som de Vera Cruz as the studio. These names appear only in that article, which relies on a Crunchyroll statement we could not open, so treat them as unconfirmed. The dub itself, however, was announced earlier, in September, by the platform itself, among 11 titles of the October season, and Ei Nerd and O Vício also mention it.

Is it worth going back?

Omelete watched the first episode before the premiere and said the animation "is not flawless" but shows a clear improvement over the first series. The episode ends leaving you wanting more, in the critic's words. It is the impression of a single outlet, which saw the episode before the public.

For anyone who does not remember everything, the first series has 170 episodes and the new run picks up directly from it. According to Wikipedia, which cites Anime News Network, the opening theme is "Kienai Riyū" by the group Wanima and the ending is "Yellow Brick Road" by One or Eight.

Who makes the anime

Studio Pierrot remains in charge, according to Wikipedia and O Vício, and this season is directed by Ayataka Tanemura. Yuki Tabata's manga was published from February 2015 to May 2026, in 38 volumes, and has passed 25 million copies in circulation, according to O Vício. In Brazil, it is published by Panini.

If any episode or dub date changes, we will update this article.

Sources: AdoroCinema (in Portuguese), O Vício (schedule) (in Portuguese), O Vício (arcs) (in Portuguese), O Vício (dub) (in Portuguese), Ei Nerd (in Portuguese), Omelete (in Portuguese) and Wikipedia (in English).
