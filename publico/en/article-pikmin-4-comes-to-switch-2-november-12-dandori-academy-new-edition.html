Pikmin 4 Comes to Switch 2 on November 12 With Dandori Academy: What the New Edition Offers

Timed trials unlocked gradually, voice commands for Oatchi and sharper visuals. The price in Brazil is still unknown.

By João GabrielNovember 11, 2026, 11:34

Pikmin 4 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy, launch trailer (Nintendo UK channel)
ShareWhatsAppXFacebookTelegram

Pikmin 4 is getting a second life on Switch 2 on November 12. Nintendo will release Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy, a version with a new trial mode, voice control for Oatchi and graphics improvements. The date appears on Nintendo's website and in four other outlets I checked: Gematsu, VideoGamer, Make Tech Easier and Instant Gaming.

Dandori Academy: 72 trials, and not all of them on day one

The new mode works like a dandori school, dandori being the art of planning the Pikmin's tasks to get more done in less time. According to Nintendo, players race to collect each stage's treasure as fast as they can. There are 72 stages in total, unlocked over time. No source says how many are available at launch, and Make Tech Easier points out that the company has not said.

The trials take place on weekends: Nintendo's website mentions exams every Friday, Saturday and Sunday, and VideoGamer, which cites the game's UK page, confirms it. There are three difficulty levels and medals that unlock rewards. VideoGamer adds two details that only it reports and that I could not verify elsewhere: practice is unlimited, but the exam allows one attempt per week, and the pace would be six stages a month, stretching the content over a year. Online leaderboards exist but, according to Nintendo's website, require a Nintendo account and a Nintendo Switch Online subscription, sold separately.

Oatchi Now Listens to You

The canine companion responds to voice commands picked up by the Switch 2's built-in microphone. All five texts say so. VideoGamer, citing the game's page, mentions nine different commands. None of the sources lists what they are.

Play With a Friend, Even if They Don't Own the Game

Dandori Battles, the game's duels, can be played with a friend on the same console or via GameShare, locally or online through GameChat, “even if they don't have the game,” according to Nintendo as quoted by Gematsu. Make Tech Easier spells out the rules of the feature: the game owner's Switch 2 starts the session, the guest can play only for as long as it lasts, and GameShare via GameChat requires a Switch 2 for each participant. The other outlets do not go into these details.

Better Visuals, but No Frame Rate

Gematsu and Make Tech Easier cite higher-resolution visuals both on TV and in handheld mode. VideoGamer goes further and mentions up to 4K on TV, a figure that it alone presents. Frame rate appears on none of the five pages, so nothing can be said about smoothness for now.

How Much It Costs and Who Needs to Buy What

Those who already own Pikmin 4 can buy just the upgrade pack, in digital format. Make Tech Easier says Nintendo's US store lists the pack at US$9.99; VideoGamer, relying on GameSpew, gives £7.99 in the UK. Those without the game will find an all-in-one bundle, but none of the sources gives its price. As for Brazil: none of the five pages mentions a price or availability in Brazilian reais, so it is worth checking the Brazilian eShop before deciding.

There is a discrepancy worth noting. Make Tech Easier, based on Nintendo's store, cites an estimated size of 1.2 GB, while VideoGamer says Nintendo lists 11.3 GB. They may refer to different items, such as the pack and the full game, but none of the pages explains. Check the size in the store before clearing space on your card.

One last tip from reading all the texts: VideoGamer, citing GameSpew, says a demo exists and that progress carries over to the full game. I did not find this information on the Nintendo page I read, so treat it as something to confirm.
