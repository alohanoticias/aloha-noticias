Can you play Football Manager 27 in Brazil? Questions and answers on the November 10 launch

Epic and the game's own help page say it is not sold or redeemable here. What is confirmed, what rests on a single source, and what nobody has explained.

By João GabrielNovember 10, 2026, 21:42

FM27 | First Look Livestream (official Football Manager channel)
ShareWhatsAppXFacebookTelegram

Football Manager 27 came out today, November 10, on PC, Mac, Xbox Series X|S and PS5, with mobile versions through Netflix and Apple Arcade. For readers in Brazil, the question that matters more is a different one: can you actually buy and play it? According to Epic and the game's own help page, not through official channels. Below is what is confirmed, what comes from a single source, and what nobody has explained so far.

When does it come out, and in which versions?

The official FM27 page lists four versions: PC and Mac (Steam, Epic Games Store and Microsoft Store), console (Xbox and PS5), mobile through Netflix, and Touch through Apple Arcade. The November 10 launch date shows up in lists from Canaltech and Overdrive, both dated November 6. Players who pre-ordered the digital version got 10% off and early access on PC and Mac until November 10. There was also a free trial from October 29 to November 3, running 6 pm to 6 pm London time (GMT). Simple math: Brazil's capital, Brasília, is three hours behind GMT, so locally that was 3 pm to 3 pm.

Is the game sold in Brazil?

Epic's support page is blunt: Football Manager is not available for purchase or play on the Epic Games Store in Brazil because of licensing restrictions. The Football Manager FAQ says something similar about the codes printed inside the box: the game "is not available for redemption or activation in Brazil." That makes two official pages pointing the same way, one about Epic's store and one about code redemption.

What they do not say is whether Steam and the Microsoft Store treat Brazilian accounts differently. The official pages we read also give no price in Brazilian reais. For that, the only route is to open the game's page in the store tied to your account and see whether a buy button exists.

What about Game Pass?

Overdrive, in a piece dated November 6, says Microsoft confirmed in an Xbox Wire Brasil statement of November 3 that FM27 will not be part of Game Pass in Brazil. That is a single source: we could not open the statement, so we treat it as likely, not final. The free trial for PC Game Pass and Ultimate subscribers applied "in eligible markets," and an earlier Overdrive article, from October 22, warned readers not to assume Brazil was included. That trial is over. For what comes and goes on the service this month, see our Game Pass November article.

Why is Brazil left out?

Epic cites "licensing restrictions" and nothing more. Overdrive recalls, as background, that the series has run into player image rights and the use of club names and badges for years. No official page explains the specific FM27 case, so the detailed reason remains interpretation, not confirmed information.

And what about Brazilian club names?

On October 29, Overdrive reported on a fan-made file called Real Names Licence Fix, published on FM Scout. It is not an official patch from Sports Interactive, the developer. According to the article, when the license files are removed from the game, Brazilian clubs appear as abbreviations (FLA instead of Flamengo, COR instead of Corinthians), and the file swaps those for real names, without badges, player photos or kits. This too is a single source, and we tested nothing. The article itself recommends backing up the game folder and checking the installer before running it.

What to do before paying?

This part is our own reading, with no official source behind it. Before spending money, check in your store whether the purchase really goes through, and in which currency, and read the platform's help page. Keys sold by third parties call for extra care: if the FAQ says redeeming a physical code is not possible in Brazil, there is no reason to assume a loose key will work on your account. When in doubt, wait for an official clarification.

What nobody has explained yet

The price in reais is missing: Overdrive says no official figure has been announced for the Brazilian market. It is also unclear whether sales could open here later, and whether the Mobile (Netflix) and Touch (Apple Arcade) versions reach Brazilian accounts. As of November 10, when we wrote this, we found no official answer on any of the three.
