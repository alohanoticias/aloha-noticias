GTA 6 takes to the streets: from the Vice City sign in Miami to billboards in São Paulo

Two weeks before launch, Rockstar has spread the game across cities in several countries. Here is what is confirmed, what was only spotted on the street and what caused a stir.

By João GabrielNovember 6, 2026, 19:16

Grand Theft Auto VI, Trailer 2 (PlayStation channel)
ShareWhatsAppXFacebookTelegram

GTA 6 is two weeks away, and Rockstar has already taken the game outdoors. In Miami, the city that inspires Vice City, the marketing became a giant rooftop sign, murals and even a blimp. In São Paulo, billboards started showing up in late October. We pulled together what is confirmed, what was only spotted around town and what stirred things up. The game launches November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

Miami has turned into Vice City

The most visible symbol is a pink “Welcome to Vice City” sign, lowered by helicopter on September 22 onto the roof of the Kaseya Center, the Miami Heat's arena. According to Forbes, Rockstar paid Miami-Dade County $975,000 for the spot. The structure covers about 4,690 square feet (roughly 436 m²) and is set to stay until April 11, 2027, the Heat's last home game.

Forbes and Fortune both point out one detail: the price came in $25,000 under the $1 million threshold that would have required a county commission vote, and Mayor Daniella Levine Cava approved the deal on her own.

The rest of the package, according to Vice and Fortune: ten murals with game artwork at the Wynwood Walls open-air museum, with free admission for county residents through 2028; a Goodyear blimp in Vice City livery; cars modified to look like the ones in the game, with plates from the fictional state of Leonida; and the Miami Heat's “A Night in Vice City” game on November 18, the eve of launch, against the Milwaukee Bucks.

Not everyone liked the idea

The campaign caused friction. According to Fortune, on September 3 Sheriff Rosie Cordero-Stutz and Commissioner Juan Carlos Bermudez urged the county to refuse to use public resources for the game, in a statement that ends with “Miami-Dade County is not Vice City.” Forbes says a proposal to rebrand Miami International Airport as “Vice City Airport” was reportedly rejected, with the sheriff citing the game's violence against law enforcement.

Fortune's October 19 piece showed the county commission about to vote on the matter on October 20. We could not find the result of that vote, so we will not say what was decided.

And in the rest of the United States?

Vice cites, based on social media posts and artist credits, a giant mural of Jason and Lucia on Hollywood Boulevard in Los Angeles, a hand-painted mural in Brooklyn made by Colossal Media, and double-decker buses carrying game ads in New York and Chicago.

In Brazil, the billboards reached São Paulo

According to Critical Hits, in a November 4 report, digital GTA 6 billboards appeared on Avenida Nove de Julho, near Praça da Sé, and around Lapa. The first known sighting dates from October 27, in photos shared by users on social media. There are two styles: one inspired by the game's cover, with characters and scenes from Vice City, and a simpler one with Jason and Lucia next to the release date. The panels carry the Eletromidia brand, an outdoor media company. The site stresses that there is no official survey of the locations.

Viciados, on November 5, goes further: it says that since the 4th, São Paulo subway stations have been decked out with posters and giant panels for the game, naming Consolação, Sé, Palmeiras-Barra Funda, República, Vila Sônia and Luz, plus ads on streaming and free-to-air TV, including SBT. Since only one of the two sites carries these details, treat them with caution until Rockstar or PlayStation weigh in. Critical Hits says no campaign has been confirmed in other Brazilian cities.

What is official and what was only spotted

Vice draws this line. Actions presented as official partnerships, such as free admission to the Wynwood Walls, sit on one side. On the other are the blimp, the car convoy and the Los Angeles and Brooklyn murals, described as spotted or reported, without direct confirmation from Rockstar. In practice: when you see a photo of a billboard on social media, check whether the company has confirmed it before passing it on as an official campaign. If anything new turns up in Brazil, we will update this story.
