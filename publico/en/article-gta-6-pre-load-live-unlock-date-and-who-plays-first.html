GTA 6 is downloading now: it unlocks on November 19, and New Zealand plays 16 hours before Brazil

Xbox opened the pre-load first, PS5 followed at local midnight. What it takes, when the game unlocks and which country starts first.

By Aloha TeamNovember 10, 2026, 13:57

ShareWhatsAppXFacebookTelegram

GTA 6 is already downloading. Since Monday evening (Nov 9) in Brazil, Xbox Series X|S owners who bought the digital version have been able to grab the files, and on PS5 the pre-load opened at midnight on Tuesday (Nov 10). Just don't stare at the progress bar hoping to play: the game only opens on November 19.

Why did Xbox go first?

The two platforms run on different clocks. According to Rockstar, the Xbox pre-load opened worldwide at the same moment, 12 a.m. GMT on November 10. Brasília is three hours behind, so in Brazil that was 9 p.m. on Monday. On PS5, each region unlocks at its own midnight. In practice, Xbox players in Brazil got a three-hour head start.

The file is big. Rockstar says up to 212 GB, depending on the console. That is more than many people have free after installing a handful of large games, so check your storage before leaving the download running.

How to set it up

On PS5, open the Game Library, select GTA 6 and go to the countdown: that is where you can check whether the content is set to download automatically. On Xbox, the download starts on its own once the pre-load opens, as long as the console is set up for it. Either way, leave the console on or in rest mode and online. If the bar gets stuck, pausing and resuming usually gets it moving.

What about boxed copies? Rockstar says code-in-box versions should reach retailers from November 12. There is no game disc inside, just a code that has to be redeemed before the download can start.

When can you play?

On Thursday, November 19. The unlock follows midnight in each store region. In Brazil, the game opens at 12 a.m. on the 19th, Brasília time. Until then, even at 100% installed, it stays locked on the console.

Who plays first?

New Zealand. Thanks to its time zone, it reaches midnight on the 19th before anyone else, at 11:00 UTC on November 18. In Brasília that is 8 a.m. on Wednesday. In other words, Brazil waits 16 hours longer than New Zealand.

From there the unlock sweeps across the map. Converted to Brasília time, all still on November 18: Australia (Sydney) at 10 a.m., Japan and South Korea at noon, India at 3:30 p.m., the UAE at 5 p.m., France, Germany, Spain and Italy at 8 p.m., and the UK at 9 p.m.

The United States and Canada unlock at midnight New York time, which is 2 a.m. on the 19th in Brazil. On the US West Coast, local clocks still read 9 p.m. on the 18th. Mexico and Central America close out the list at 3 a.m. Brasília time. One caveat: these tables were compiled by specialist sites from store listings; Rockstar has not published a country-by-country list.

What else is official

The game is finished. On Monday (Nov 9), Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick said on the earnings call that development is complete. That is the so-called "gold" status, when the final version is locked for distribution. After two delays, it removes the fear of yet another date change.

On consoles the target is 30 frames per second, PS5 Pro included, according to Rockstar. The studio says the goal is to keep the density of streets, traffic and crowds without hurting stability.

On November 19 itself, Rockstar will also add a free printable cover art poster and a map of Leonida to the official site. A double-sided physical version will be sold at the Rockstar Store shortly after launch for US$0.99, or come free with any purchase there.

What we still don't know

Rockstar hasn't explained what the PS5 Pro version actually changes, or whether a higher frame rate mode could arrive later. The PC version still has no date. For players in Brazil the plan is short: free up space, leave the console downloading and wait for Wednesday night to turn into Thursday.
