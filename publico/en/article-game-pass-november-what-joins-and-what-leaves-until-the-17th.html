Game Pass: 14 games leave by the 15th, and Toxic Commando arrives on the 12th

Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2 and GoldenEye 007 are on their way out; Warhammer Survivors lands on launch day. The day-by-day schedule for November's first wave.

By Aloha TeamNovember 9, 2026, 11:26

ShareWhatsAppXFacebookTelegram

Game Pass subscribers have a busy week ahead. New games from the month's first wave keep arriving until Tuesday, November 17, and on Sunday the 15th the library loses 13 titles at once, including Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2 and GoldenEye 007. Here are the dates, so you can sort out your backlog before the deadline.

Microsoft published the list on Xbox Wire on November 3, and outlets such as Omelete, Einerd and Pure Xbox picked it up. The game count changes from site to site: some say 15, others reach 23 once the Essential classics are added. Every date in this piece matches in at least two sources.

What is still coming until the 17th

On Tuesday the 10th, Warhammer Survivors, the console version of Football Manager 27 and Tanuki: Pon's Summer arrive. Wednesday the 11th brings Thronefall and Hope in the City. John Carpenter's Toxic Commando lands on Thursday the 12th, and the wave wraps up on Tuesday the 17th with Aphelion and PAW Patrol: Dino World.

If you opened the app early in the month, you already found Football Manager 27 (PC), Pit of Goblin, Vampire Crawlers, Void/Breaker, Escape Academy 2, Firefighting Simulator: Ignite, MySims Cozy Bundle. All of them joined between November 3 and 5.

Toxic Commando is the wave's biggest name

The co-op shooter from Saber Interactive, the studio behind *Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2*, came out on March 12, 2026 for PS5, Xbox Series X|S and PC. It joins the subscription exactly eight months later. The pitch is simple: up to four friends take on zombie hordes across open maps, with trucks, mud and plenty of explosions, in an open tribute to John Carpenter's 1980s action and horror films. The director even lent his voice to one of the trailers.

According to Pure Xbox and Tech Insider, Toxic Commando will be available on Ultimate, Premium and PC Game Pass. If you wanted to try it with friends without paying full price, this is the best chance so far.

Warhammer Survivors joins on launch day

The other highlight is Warhammer Survivors, which hits the library the same day it reaches stores. It follows the *Vampire Survivors* formula: your character attacks automatically, the screen fills with enemies, and the fun is in picking the right upgrades each level. That is no accident. Auroch Digital, the team behind *Warhammer 40,000: Boltgun*, made it together with poncle, the creator of *Vampire Survivors*. Outside the subscription it costs US$7.99, according to Gematsu. Based on the published lists, it lands on Ultimate and PC Game Pass, but not Premium.

What leaves on the 15th (and one that goes earlier)

If you are halfway through a Football Manager 26 career, you have less time: it leaves on Tuesday the 10th, the same day the console version of 27 arrives. The other 13 leave on Sunday the 15th: 1000xRESIST, Barbie Horse Trails, Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2, Firefighting Simulator: The Squad, GoldenEye 007, Great God Grove, Moonlighter, Pigeon Simulator, Police Simulator: Patrol Officers, Relic Hunters Legend, Total Chaos, Winter Burrow and Wreckfest.

The departure that hurts most is Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2, which turns ten in 2026, as Oficina da Net points out. GoldenEye 007, which according to Notebookcheck had been in the library since January 2023, is also going. One detail: it only runs on console and cloud, not on PC.

While a game is still in the library, subscribers can buy it at a discount. Outlets mention up to 20%, but the discount does not apply to every title or every plan, so check the store price before you decide.

Classics on Essential

On the same Sunday, the cheapest plan, Essential, gets eight older games: Banjo-Kazooie, Banjo-Tooie, Banjo-Kazooie: Nuts & Bolts, Gears of War: Judgment, Halo: Spartan Assault, Perfect Dark and both Viva Piñata games. For entry-level subscribers, it is the most tangible news of the month.

November's second wave has not been announced yet. It usually arrives in the second half of the month, together with the list of games leaving at the end of November.
