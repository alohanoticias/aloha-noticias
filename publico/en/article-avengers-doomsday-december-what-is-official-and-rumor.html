Avengers: Doomsday arrives in December: what is official and what is still rumor

A trailer, a huge cast, Robert Downey Jr. as the villain and the Russo brothers back. We separate what is confirmed from internet guesswork.

By João GabrielNovember 6, 2026, 10:29

Avengers: Doomsday, official trailer (Marvel Brasil channel, Portuguese dub)
ShareWhatsAppXFacebookTelegram

A little over a month away from Avengers: Doomsday (released in Brazil as *Vingadores: Doutor Destino*), set to open in Brazilian theaters on December 17, 2026. With this much news going around, it is easy to lose track of what is an announcement and what is rumor. Let's take it in parts.

What is official

The film is directed by Anthony and Joe Russo, the same duo behind *Infinity War* and *Endgame*. The script is by Michael Waldron and Stephen McFeely, according to Omelete. And the villain of the title is played by Robert Downey Jr., who returns to the Marvel universe as Doctor Doom. For now, the film's official page on Disney's website credits the script to Stephen McFeely alone.

The first full trailer came out on July 20, along with a poster featuring the villain, according to Ingresso.com. After that, Marvel released more material in August, around the time ticket sales opened abroad.

How long is it?

Anthony Russo told Fandango it is “a two-and-a-half-hour movie, more or less,” according to Omelete. Ticket-sales sites list about 2h45 (165 minutes), so the figures differ a little. If the final number lands near 165 minutes, it will be the second-longest of the big Avengers crossover films: *Endgame* runs past three hours, and *Infinity War* is 2h29.

What the newest teaser shows

On October 24, Omelete reported a new teaser with about 30 seconds of new scenes showing the heroes interacting, with Thor and Steve Rogers in the article's headline. The text does not describe the scenes, so there is not much more to say without watching the official video.

A cast that mixes generations

The list released by Marvel brings together three groups. The usual Avengers, such as Chris Evans, Chris Hemsworth, Anthony Mackie, Sebastian Stan and Paul Rudd. Names from the more recent MCU, among them Simu Liu, Florence Pugh, Pedro Pascal, Vanessa Kirby and Letitia Wright. And the Fox-era X-Men, with Patrick Stewart, Ian McKellen, Kelsey Grammer, Rebecca Romijn and others.

Omelete also lists Winston Duke, Danny Ramirez, Joseph Quinn, Ebon Moss-Bachrach and Tenoch Huerta in the cast, plus James Marsden among the Fox-era X-Men actors. Cast lists change from article to article, so the official film page remains the reference.

It is rare to see this many people from different phases in the same film, and that is exactly what explains the hype.

What is still rumor

Before each trailer, insider rumors about dates and content appear. In August, for instance, Omelete reported that an insider (Daniel Richtman) pointed to a new trailer alongside the start of ticket sales, but made it clear that Marvel had not confirmed anything. That is the pattern: treat it as a guess until the official announcement.

There are also supposed leaks and AI-recreated trailers going around. If it did not come from Marvel's official channel, be skeptical.

Quick questions

When does it open? December 17, 2026 in Brazil, in theaters only. Dates in other countries may differ, so check your local listings.

Can I watch a trailer already? Yes, and Marvel Brasil's official channel has the Portuguese-dubbed version (below).

Do I need to rewatch the earlier films? No official source lists that as required. Do not let anyone sell you a rule that does not exist.

Is there a date for a new trailer? So far, none confirmed by Marvel.

A note on the date: Marvel's official page lists December 18, which is the US release. In Brazil the film opens a day earlier, on December 17, as Ingresso.com and Omelete report. Dates in other countries may differ.
