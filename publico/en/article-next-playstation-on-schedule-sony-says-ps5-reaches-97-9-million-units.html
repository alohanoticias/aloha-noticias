Next PlayStation on schedule, Sony says; PS5 reaches 97.9 million units

Sony's chief financial officer addressed the new generation while presenting the July-September results. Name, price, specs and date remain unannounced.

By Aloha TeamNovember 5, 2026, 19:23

ShareWhatsAppXFacebookTelegram

Sony released its July-September 2026 financial results on November 5, and amid the spreadsheets came a line that matters to players: the next console is on schedule. The remark comes from Lin Tao, the company's chief financial officer, answering a question about the new generation. Hardware.com.br, citing Video Games Chronicle, quotes her as saying: “We are progressing according to schedule.”

What Sony said about the new console

That was all, and it is not much. Sony did not announce a name, price, specifications or release date, as Hardware.com.br and GamersRD both note. The latter, a Spanish-language site, carries a second statement attributed to the same executive: in preparing for the next-generation platform, the company will keep its focus on consolidating preparations and investing the necessary resources. The two reports agree on the essentials, that development is on plan, and differ only in wording, as happens when each outlet translates its own way a remark made in English.

Two details are concrete. The first is memory: both outlets say Sony has already agreed with suppliers on the quantity it needs for the next fiscal year. GamersRD stresses that the company disclosed neither volumes nor supplier names. The second is the spending calendar: according to Hardware.com.br, Sony intends to step up preparations in the fiscal year beginning in April 2027. That date appears only in that report and I could not confirm it elsewhere, so it should be treated with caution.

And the launch? No official date. Hardware.com.br recalls that analyst estimates exist, such as one from Piers Harding-Rolls of Ampere Analysis, who in July pointed to late 2028 as the most likely scenario. That is a market guess, not a Sony plan, and the report itself makes this clear.

The PS5 in numbers

The current console has reached 97.9 million units since launch, a figure that appears in all three sources I read. Between July and September the total was about 2.6 million. Hardware.com.br compares that with 4 million in the same period of 2025 and speaks of a 35% drop. That comparison comes only from that report, but the simple math checks out: 2.6 is 65% of 4.

A caution on reading these figures: what Sony reports is units shipped to retailers, not sold to consumers. Hardware.com.br and the body of the GamersRD text speak of units “shipped,” while O Vício writes that the company “sold” 2.6 million and GamersRD's headline uses “sold.” For anyone following the market, the difference matters, because stock sitting on a shop shelf counts in the first tally and not in the second.

The player side shows up too. There were 127 million monthly active users in September, a figure reported by Hardware.com.br and O Vício, and the former adds that it is a record for the period. Marvel's Wolverine reached 2.4 million copies, again in both sources.

On profit, the sources disagree. Hardware.com.br reports an 83% rise in operating income for the games and network services segment, and attributes part of it to the absence of one-off expenses that weighed on the same quarter of 2025. GamersRD reports an 89% increase in operating income, without saying for which division, and cites the box-office performance of Spider-Man: Brand New Day. Since I did not check Sony's own report to settle it, I will not pick either number.

What this changes today

For anyone who owns or is thinking of buying a PS5, nothing changes now. The useful information is an absence: there is no price or date to plan around, and any number circulating before a Sony announcement is a third-party estimate. What remains open is precisely the essentials: the device's name, how much it will cost, what it delivers and when it reaches stores.

Sources: Hardware.com.br (in Portuguese), O Vício (in Portuguese) and GamersRD (in Spanish).
