Privacy policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026. This page explains, in plain words, what data is processed when you visit ALOHA NOTÍCIAS (alohanoticias.com), why, and how you control it. We follow Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13,709/2018).

Who is responsible

ALOHA NOTÍCIAS is responsible for the data processing described here. For any privacy matter, write to contato@alohanoticias.com.

What we don't collect

The site has no sign-up and no reader login. The only place we ask for details is the form on the contact page, and only if you choose to write to us.

Visit statistics (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics 4 to see how many people read each page, on what kind of device, from which country or approximate city, and where the visit came from (a search, a social network, our Telegram channel or a partner link, identified by tags in the address). Google Analytics uses cookies and browser identifiers. We receive aggregated numbers and do not use this data to identify you.

In the European Union, the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, measurement only starts after you click “Accept” in the cookie notice. In other countries, including Brazil, it starts when the page loads, based on our legitimate interest in understanding how the site is used. Anywhere, you can turn it off with “Decline” in the notice or under “Cookie preferences” in the footer. Data is kept for the retention period set in Google Analytics. Learn more about how Google uses data from partner sites.

Hosting and security (Cloudflare)

The site is hosted by Cloudflare. To deliver pages and protect the site from attacks, Cloudflare processes technical data from each request, such as IP address, browser and page requested, and may count visits in aggregate, without cookies.

Our own visit count

Besides Google Analytics, the site itself counts page views, without cookies. We record the page opened, the site or link the visit came from (including the tags in the address), the country, the type of device and the page language. To know how many different people came each day, the IP address and browser are turned into a scrambled code using a key that changes every day and is deleted the next day. The IP is not stored, and this code cannot identify anyone or recognize the same person on another day.

What stays in your browser

We store in your own browser (local storage, not sent to us) your light or dark theme choice, your answer to the cookie notice and which items in the news bell you have already seen. You can erase all of it by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Notifications

If you turn on alerts for new articles, the OneSignal service stores an identifier of your browser to send them. Nothing is turned on without your click, and you can switch it off anytime in your browser or phone settings.

Content from other sites

Fonts come from Google Fonts. Some photos are shown from their original address (for example Wikimedia Commons, Unsplash or studio and press websites), and videos are embedded from YouTube in enhanced privacy mode (youtube-nocookie.com). When these items load, those services receive your IP address and follow their own privacy rules. Share buttons are plain links: they only send something to a social network if you click them.

Ads (Google AdSense)

The site shows Google AdSense ads, which keep the content free. Google and its partners may use cookies and device identifiers to show ads, including personalized ones, cap how often they repeat and measure results. Ads only load after you click “Accept” in the cookie notice; if you decline, the site works normally without ads. For visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, consent is requested through a Google-certified consent management platform. You can change your choice under “Cookie preferences” in the footer and manage personalization at adssettings.google.com. See how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies, such as the DoubleClick cookie, enables it and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.

International transfers

Google, Cloudflare and OneSignal may process data on servers outside Brazil, with the safeguards set out in their own policies and in the LGPD.

Messages and emails you send us

If you write to us, through the form or by email, we use your name and email only to reply. Form messages are stored on our server (Cloudflare) for up to one year and then deleted; to prevent abuse we also keep a scrambled code that cannot identify you. We do not sell or share data with anyone.

Your rights

Under the LGPD you can ask us to confirm whether we process your data, to access, correct, anonymize or delete it, to tell you who it was shared with, and to withdraw consent. Write to contato@alohanoticias.com. You can also complain to Brazil's National Data Protection Authority (ANPD) or to the authority in your country.

Children

We do not knowingly ask for or collect data that identifies children or teenagers. If you are a parent or guardian and believe we received a minor's data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

When something changes in how the site works, we update this page and the date at the top. The Portuguese version prevails if there is any difference.
