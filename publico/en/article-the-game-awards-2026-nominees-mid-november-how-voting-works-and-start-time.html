The Game Awards 2026: nominees arrive in mid-November; here's how voting works and when to watch

The ceremony takes place on December 10 in Los Angeles. Only games released by November 20 can compete, and fans account for 10% of the final vote.

By João GabrielNovember 10, 2026, 19:56

The Game Awards Orchestra performs music from the 2025 Game of the Year nominees (official The Game Awards channel)
ShareWhatsAppXFacebookTelegram

Gaming's awards season has a start date. The Game Awards 2026 takes place on Thursday, December 10, at the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles, and the nominees are expected in mid-November, according to the show's official website. In other words, the Game of the Year race is about to take shape.

The date was confirmed on social media by the show's creator and host, Geoff Keighley. In his August post, he said more details and tickets would come in the fall. The Peacock Theater has hosted the show since 2023 and, according to Argentine outlet Cultura Geek, seats about 7,100 people.

Which games can compete

The cutoff is already on the show's FAQ page: a game must be on sale by Friday, November 20, 2026, at 9 p.m. Pacific Time. For Players' Voice, the only category decided entirely by fans, the deadline is November 30.

That date has driven a lot of conversation in recent weeks, because GTA 6 is set to launch on November 19, one day before the cutoff. Under the rules, it can be nominated. Being eligible does not guarantee a nomination, though: jurors still need to have played enough to vote.

How winners are chosen

There are two stages. First, more than 100 media outlets and content creators from several countries each submit a ballot with their top five picks per category. The games that appear on the most ballots become the nominees; ties can push a category to six or more.

Then comes the final vote. The jury accounts for 90% of the result and fans for 10%. Fans vote on the official site, TheGameAwards.com, and voting stays open until December 9 at 6 p.m. Pacific Time, the day before the show.

What happened in 2025

Last year, the nominees were announced on November 17 in a livestream, and the ceremony took place on December 11. The big winner was Clair Obscur: Expedition 33, the debut RPG from French studio Sandfall Interactive. It won Game of the Year and took nine trophies in total, the most in the show's history, ahead of the seven won by The Last of Us Part II in 2020.

In the main race, Clair Obscur beat Death Stranding 2: On the Beach, Donkey Kong Bananza, Hades II, Hollow Knight: Silksong and Kingdom Come: Deliverance II. Besides Game of the Year, it won awards such as Best Game Direction, Best Narrative, Best Art Direction, Best Independent Game and Best Debut Indie Game. Of the categories it was nominated in, it only lost Best Audio Design, which went to Battlefield 6, and the fan vote.

The Players' Voice award, picked by fans alone, went to Wuthering Waves. The finalists in that category are usually revealed after the rest: in 2025, they came out on December 1.

Start time and where to watch

The broadcast starts at 7:30 p.m. Eastern Time (4:30 p.m. Pacific) on Thursday, December 10. In Brazil it is 9:30 p.m., and in the UK and Portugal it is already 12:30 a.m. on Friday. The show is free and streams live on The Game Awards' official channels, including YouTube and Twitch; no ticket or subscription is needed.

Beyond the trophies, the night usually brings new announcements and trailers, the so-called world premieres, plus performances by the show's orchestra, like the one in the video above. Which games will be revealed on stage has not been announced. Once the nominees are out, check which categories open for voting on day one.
