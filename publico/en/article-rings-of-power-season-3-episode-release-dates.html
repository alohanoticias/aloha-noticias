The Rings of Power, season 3: when each episode drops on Prime Video

The premiere is November 11 with four episodes at once. Here are the season's three release dates and how to plan your viewing.

By João GabrielNovember 7, 2026, 10:26

The Rings of Power, official season 3 trailer (Prime Video Brasil channel)
ShareWhatsAppXFacebookTelegram

The third season of The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power arrives on Prime Video on November 11, and the schedule has changed from what the series used to do. Instead of one episode a week from the start, the platform will release episodes in three batches, according to Omelete.

The schedule

November 11: the first four episodes, all at once.

November 18: episodes 5 and 6.

November 25: episodes 7 and 8, which close the season.

There are eight episodes in total. The dates come from Omelete, in a July 24 article, and the same schedule appears in its October 21 piece on the trailer and posters. Amazon itself confirmed the schedule in an official announcement and says the season premieres on Prime Video in more than 240 countries and territories.

Where the story left off

According to Catraca Livre, the new season jumps five years ahead of the second, after the fall of Eregion, when Sauron's armies advanced across much of Middle-earth. The territories that remain free, according to the text, are Khazad-dûm, where the dwarves face threats deep in the mountains, and Lindon and Rivendell, protected by the power of the Three Rings.

Also according to Catraca Livre, Sauron is growing stronger in Mordor, in the newly built Barad-dûr, seeking the power to carry out his plans, and the peoples must set their rivalries aside to face the common enemy. Gandalf continues his journey in Rhûn, trying to change the course of the conflict, and Númenor is on the brink of civil war. The story is set in the Second Age, thousands of years before *The Hobbit* and *The Lord of the Rings*.

What changes for viewers

With four episodes at once, people who like to binge have plenty of material at launch. Those who want to avoid spoilers for longer will need to be careful on social media during the week of November 11. After that, the wait between batches is one week.

And the trailer?

The season's trailer came out on October 21. According to Critical Hits, it shows the forging of the One Ring and features Simon Pegg as the Balrog. The official video, from Prime Video Brasil's channel, is just below.

Still on the trailer, Catraca Livre describes Galadriel fighting Khamûl, the Easterling, before being knocked from her horse, along with glimpses of winged creatures, the Nazgûl and Prince Durin trying to keep the dwarves united. The video ends with Sauron before the forged Ring, its inscription beginning to glow.

Do I need to have seen the earlier seasons?

The sources I consulted do not address that. The series tells one continuous story, so if it has been a while since you watched the earlier seasons, a recap beforehand helps. You can find recaps on Prime Video itself.

Who is behind the series

According to Catraca Livre, Charlie Vickers returns as Sauron, Morfydd Clark as Galadriel and Robert Aramayo as Elrond. Newcomers include Jamie Campbell Bower as Celeborn, Eddie Marsan as Thrain and Andrew Richardson as Anarion. The series is run by J.D. Payne and Patrick McKay, and the directors named are Charlotte Brändström, Sanaa Hamri and Stefan Schwartz. The first and second seasons total 16 episodes, also on Prime Video.

A reminder

Streaming dates can change, so check Prime Video close to the premiere. If anything changes, we will update this article.
