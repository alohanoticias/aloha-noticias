One Piece: Clockwork Island and Chopper's Kingdom hit Brazilian theaters on December 3 and 10, dubbed

Paris Filmes postponed both classic films, which now open in back-to-back weeks in Brazil, each with a short from the franchise before the screening.

By Aloha TeamNovember 21, 2026, 18:56

One Piece: Clockwork Island Adventure, official trailer (Paris Filmes channel)
ShareWhatsAppXFacebookTelegram

Anyone hoping to see the Straw Hats in Brazilian theaters in October will have to wait a little longer. Paris Filmes moved One Piece: Clockwork Island Adventure to December 3, and One Piece: Chopper's Kingdom on the Island of Strange Animals went from December 3 to December 10. Both arrive with a Portuguese dub made specifically for this release.

The films are part of the package the distributor put together with the franchise's first three features, released in Japan between 2000 and 2002 and never shown in Brazilian theaters before. The first, One Piece: The Movie, kicked off the series in September.

The dates, film by film

Clockwork Island Adventure, from 2001, was set for October 22 and was rescheduled a few days before opening. Chopper's Kingdom, from 2002, follows a week later. Each screening comes with an extra: the short Take Aim! The Pirate Baseball King (2004) plays before Clockwork Island, and Dream Soccer King! (2002) before Chopper.

What they are about

In Clockwork Island, the Going Merry is stolen by the Trump Siblings with Nami on board, and Luffy, Zoro, Usopp and Sanji chase the ship all the way to Clockwork Island. It is a short adventure, about an hour long, from the days when the crew was still small.

In Chopper's Kingdom, the crew lands on an island inhabited by talking animals that live as a kingdom. There, Chopper is proclaimed king while hunters try to take the residents' valuable horns. The story is set in the middle of the Alabasta saga.

Do you need to have seen the anime?

No. Neither film is part of the official story of Eiichiro Oda's manga, and both plots wrap up within the film itself. Anime fans will recognize the crew early in its journey; newcomers get a short, no-commitment way in.

For moviegoers in Brazil

In the Portuguese dub, Carol Valença voices Luffy, Glauco Marques voices Zoro, Tati Keplmair voices Nami and Adrian Titini voices Usopp, according to Paris Filmes. Because the films are short, even with the opening short the screening runs under an hour and a half, a good format for kids. It is worth checking whether the theater shows the dubbed version, which is the main draw of the re-release.

For readers in other countries: in Mexico and parts of Latin America, both films also arrive in December, on their own dates.

A holiday season full of Straw Hats

The films arrive at a time of heavy exposure for the franchise. Since November 1, Fortnite has run an entire season with the crew, called Treasure Hunters, with Luffy, Zoro and Nami as outfits and even Gum-Gum Fruit powers in the game. For the distributor, it is a chance to also attract people who met the characters through the game. For longtime fans, it is the first time these 2001 and 2002 adventures can be seen on a big screen in Brazil, with theater sound and in Portuguese.

What we still don't know

Paris Filmes has not explained the delay or announced where the films will be available after their theatrical run. There is also no word on the franchise's other classic features, which number more than a dozen.
