Minecraft Wilderness Bound: what the autumn update brought, from straw beds to cushions

The third update of 2026 added a forest of colorful leaves, abandoned camps and new decorating blocks, but no new mobs.

By Aloha TeamNovember 20, 2026, 13:22

ShareWhatsAppXFacebookTelegram

Mojang named the third Minecraft update of 2026 Wilderness Bound. The name fits the content: an autumn forest, abandoned camps to explore and items for those who like decorating. It has been available for a few weeks, but the list of novelties is still worth reading for anyone who has not yet gone in.

When it came out, and the date discrepancy

Mojang's official announcement says the expedition sets off on September 15, and the PlayNews site also gives Tuesday, September 15, as the launch day. GamingOnLinux, however, records Java version 26.3 as released on September 17. The difference may come from a staggered rollout between editions and platforms, but the sources do not explain it, so the reason cannot be pinned down. On Bedrock, the same content arrived in the 26.50 line, according to Game Over Drive.

The forest of colorful leaves

The star is the Dappled Forest biome, a forest with autumn colors that can appear near cold biomes. It brings poplars, trees with red, orange or yellow leaves, with a full wood set for building. Red shrubs and shelf mushrooms also arrive; the mushrooms work as a boost for parkour and, according to the official page, also go into stews.

Abandoned camps

The Abandoned Camps are structures with a tent and a campfire, with chests and barrels of loot, and they appear in 18 biomes, according to Game Over Drive. Some explorer maps found in them point to other camps or existing structures. The idea is to give the player a clue about who passed through before, without needing any new mob: the update, in fact, adds none.

Straw bed and cushions

The straw bed lets the player skip the night without changing the respawn point. It is consumable, lasts one night and does not work in the Nether or the End, and is made from three hay bales, which yields four beds, according to Game Over Drive. Cushions, in 16 colors, are made from three wool slabs of the same color or found in the camps, and serve as seats around the campfire.

Blocks and Realms

For builders, the update adds wool and concrete stairs and slabs in all 16 colors. In Realms, the Java version gained up to five invite codes, which can be disabled or given an expiry date, and the maximum render distance rose from 12 to 25 chunks, again according to Game Over Drive. The official page adds that up to 10 players from any Bedrock platform can join an expedition in Realms, and that there are free items in the wardrobe and a "Haunts and Harvest" sound pack on the Marketplace.

Details that slip by

According to the official page, the shelf mushroom can grow into a large mushroom with bone meal, and red shrubs keep their color when planted in other biomes, which makes them useful for decorating outside the forest. The abandoned camp maps also have a new design. Mojang notes in the announcement that the features require Minecraft Bedrock or Java and may change during development.

Technical details

GamingOnLinux lists other changes in the Java version: spectator mode now uses portals, the game gained support for the Gothic language and, on Linux, prefers Wayland when available and uses SDL3 for windows and input.
