Xenoblade Chronicles 3 gets a Switch 2 edition on December 3: what changes and what it costs

The edition runs at 60 frames per second, reaches 4K on TV, adds a new heroine and a wave-based mode. Existing owners pay $9.99 for the upgrade pack.

By João GabrielNovember 20, 2026, 19:41

Xenoblade Chronicles 3 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition – Nintendo Direct 9.9.2026 (Nintendo of America channel)
ShareWhatsAppXFacebookTelegram

The RPG Xenoblade Chronicles 3, released for Switch in 2022, gets a Switch 2 edition on December 3. It is the last of three series editions Monolith Soft prepared for the new console: the first game's came out in June and Xenoblade Chronicles 2's in July.

The other two editions' dates help explain the pace: Xenoblade Chronicles: Definitive Edition hit the eShop on June 9 and physical stores on July 30; Xenoblade Chronicles 2 came out on the eShop on July 30 and on cartridge on October 1. The third arrives in both formats on the same day.

If you've never played it

Xenoblade Chronicles 3 is set in Aionios, a world split between two warring nations, Keves and Agnus, whose soldiers live short cycles of battle. The story follows Noah and Mio, from opposite sides, who end up fighting together. Combat is real-time, with a party of six characters plus a guest Hero.

What improves

On TV the game reaches 4K resolution, and in handheld mode it runs at Full HD, always at 60 frames per second, according to Nintendo. Story cutscenes are smoother and sharper too. You need a compatible TV to see 4K.

Another change: event scenes that used to be text-only are now fully voiced, in English and Japanese.

What's new

The edition adds a brand-new heroine, Shimmer, with her own quest. She fights with rapid combos and powerful strikes and has the Foresight ability, which lets her see the future. She was shown in the September 9 Nintendo Direct.

There's also a new mode, Heroes' Vault. The party faces waves of enemies; after each victory you can upgrade abilities or recruit allies before taking on a final boss. Outlets such as Siliconera and Game Informer compared it to games like Vampire Survivors.

What it costs

In the United States, the full edition costs $69.99, according to Nintendo and retailers such as Target. Anyone who already owns Xenoblade Chronicles 3 on Switch can buy just the upgrade pack for $9.99 and play the new version on Switch 2. The price in Brazil's eShop was not yet listed at the time of writing.

It's a big download: Nintendo of America's page estimates 42.6 GB. Check your console or card storage before the 3rd.

No Portuguese

An important detail for Brazilian players: the language list on Nintendo's store includes Japanese, English, French, German, Italian, Spanish, Korean and Chinese, but not Portuguese.

And the expansion?

The Xenoblade Chronicles 3 Expansion Pass, which includes the extra story Future Redeemed, is still sold separately.

Playing on Switch 1

The Switch 2 edition's physical card also works on the original Switch, but there it runs the old version without any of the new features. In other words, the new heroine, the new mode and 60 frames per second are Switch 2 only.
