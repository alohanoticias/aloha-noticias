Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World launches December 3 with over 500 monsters and Dragon Quest V's heroines

Square Enix's new monster-collecting and fusing game arrives simultaneously on Switch, Switch 2, PS5, Xbox and PC. Here is the story, what changes and the editions.

By João GabrielNovember 13, 2026, 19:24

Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World, release date trailer (official Dragon Quest channel)
ShareWhatsAppXFacebookTelegram

The Dragon Quest series where monsters are the stars of your party is back. Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World launches on Thursday, December 3, worldwide and simultaneously, on Switch, Switch 2, PS5, Xbox Series X|S and PC (Steam and Microsoft Store), according to Gematsu.

The game is published by Square Enix and developed by Tose, and in Japan it is called Dragon Quest Monsters 4, as RPG Site points out. The platform list stands out too: according to Push Square, it is the first time in decades that a Dragon Quest Monsters game comes to a PlayStation console.

Dragon Quest V's heroines

This time, the adventure is led by Bianca and Nera, two key characters from Dragon Quest V, originally released in 1992. You can switch between them or stick with one. In the story, according to Gematsu, they search for the Leaves of Life to heal their sick fathers.

A creature named Fluffy leads them to the Kingdom of Witherwood, where the leaves used to grow. But the kingdom is devastated and the leaves are nearly gone. Fluffy then gives them the power to befriend monsters, and the mission becomes bringing the place back to life. Debora, Nera's older sister, also joins the story.

Recruit, fuse and train

The series' core remains the same. There are more than 500 monsters to befriend, including franchise veterans and new ones, according to Gematsu. They can be recruited during battles, and some even ask to join. With synthesis, two monsters become a new, stronger one. In combat you can give direct orders or choose tactics.

The world has year-round summer beaches, plains with giant fruit and vegetables, ocean floors with coral and a sunken pirate ship, plus dungeons. The day and night cycle changes which monsters appear, which encourages returning to places at different times. The Monsters series began in 1998 on Game Boy, and the previous game, Dragon Quest Monsters: The Dark Prince, came out in 2023 on Switch only. Now the franchise is aiming at several platforms at once.

On the PlayStation Store, the game's page lists optional online play for up to four players with a PS Plus subscription.

Editions and bonuses

Besides the standard version, there is a Digital Deluxe Edition and a Collector's Edition, both with downloadable content, according to Gematsu. Four add-on packs launch with the game, including one that turns nearby enemies into metal monsters, known for granting lots of experience.

Early buyers get bonus items: 10 Monster Munchies, 3 Bonus Balls and 5 Seeds of Skill, available until January 5, 2027, according to RPG Site. Regional prices were not in the sources we checked; it is worth checking each platform's store, since pre-orders are open.
