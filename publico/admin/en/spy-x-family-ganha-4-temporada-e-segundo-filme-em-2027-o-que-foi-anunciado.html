Spy×Family gets a fourth season and a second film, both in 2027: what was announced and what we still don't know

The news came at an event in Tokyo on Sunday, November 8, with new artwork of the Forger family. The premiere month and the film's title have not been revealed.

By Aloha TeamNovember 13, 2026, 10:12

ShareWhatsAppXFacebookTelegram

The Forger family is coming back twice over. Spy×Family will get a fourth season and a second theatrical film, both scheduled for 2027 in Japan. The announcement came on Sunday, November 8, according to Anime News Network, along with new artwork for the season.

For newcomers: the anime adapts Tatsuya Endo's manga. Loid is a spy who must build a fake family for a mission; he "adopts" Anya, a girl who reads minds, and marries Yor, who, unknown to him, is a professional assassin. None of the three knows the others' secrets, and that is where most of the humor comes from. The family is completed by Bond, a dog who can see the future. The series mixes comedy, action and family moments, and has become one of the most popular anime of recent years.

Where and how it was announced

The confirmation came at SPY×FAMILY ANIME EXTRA MISSION II, an event with the Japanese voice cast held at Belle Salle Takadanobaba in Tokyo, according to IBTimes. It was not a complete surprise: the first Extra Mission, in June 2024, was where the third season was announced, as Brazilian site Critical Hits noted when the event was scheduled.

According to French site Spiritgamer, the official account posted the news at the end of the event, at around 8 p.m. Tokyo time, saying 2027 will be "the year of Spy×Family".

What we still don't know

For now, the announcement is brief. There is no premiere month, episode count or film title, according to Spiritgamer. Nor was it said when the new season will reach audiences outside Japan. Any date circulating on social media before an official statement should be treated with suspicion. If the October pattern repeats, season 4 could premiere in the second half of 2027, but that is only a reading of the track record, not official information.

The track record gives an idea of the pace. According to Anime News Network, the first season premiered in two parts, in April and October 2022; the second in October 2023, with 12 episodes; and the third in October 2025, with 13. In other words, the gaps have been one to two years.

What about the film?

The first feature, Spy×Family Code: White, opened in Japan in December 2023 and topped the country's box office on its opening weekend, according to Anime News Network. Its story was original, written for the big screen, rather than an adaptation of a manga arc. It is not yet known whether the second film will take the same path.

In Brazil, the series streams on Crunchyroll, which has the seasons and the first film; some seasons are also on Netflix, according to Omelete. Availability varies by country, so check your local services. Until season 4 arrives, there is time to rewatch the story or start from scratch.
