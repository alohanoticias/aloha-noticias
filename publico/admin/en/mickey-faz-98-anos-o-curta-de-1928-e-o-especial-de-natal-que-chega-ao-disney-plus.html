Mickey turns 98: the 1928 short that became a birthday and the Christmas special coming to Disney+

Steamboat Willie premiered on November 18, 1928, the date Disney treats as Mickey's and Minnie's birthday. On November 27, the mouse gets his own take on Home Alone.

By Aloha TeamNovember 18, 2026, 13:38

Mickey's Home Alone, special trailer (Disney Jr. channel)
ShareWhatsAppXFacebookTelegram

Today, November 18, Mickey turns 98. The date is not the day the character was first drawn, but the premiere of Steamboat Willie in 1928 at a New York theater. That short, just over seven minutes long, is what Disney itself points to when it talks about the mouse's birthday.

And there is a present too: on November 27, Disney+ releases Mickey's Home Alone, a Christmas special that puts the world's most famous mouse in the situation from the 1990 film.

Why November 18

Steamboat Willie was not the first Mickey cartoon produced, but it was the first to reach audiences. What made it a landmark was sound: music and effects synchronized with the picture, still rare in animation that year. Audiences heard Mickey whistling at the wheel of a steamboat, and the character took off from there.

According to Disney, the date also counts for Minnie, because the short shows the two meeting for the first time. Another detail the company's site points out: Walt Disney himself voiced Mickey until 1946.

A villain older than the hero

The one at the helm before Mickey is the captain, known in Brazil as João Bafo de Onça (Pete in English). He already existed in Disney cartoons before 1928, which in practice makes him an older member of the gang than Mickey himself. In Steamboat Willie he chews tobacco, a scene that would feel odd in a children's cartoon today.

The short that became free

On January 1, 2024, the Steamboat Willie version entered the public domain in the United States after 95 years of protection, as Duke University's Center for the Study of the Public Domain explains. That applies only to the 1928 Mickey, black-and-white and gloveless. The modern character, with the colors and look we know, remains protected, and the Mickey trademark is still Disney's. That is why images from the short can be used freely, including here.

The Christmas special

Mickey's Home Alone premieres on Disney+ on November 27, according to the service's schedule in Brazil. In the story, the gang travels for the holidays and ends up leaving Mickey and Pluto home alone. The two have to defend Christmas dinner from two intruders, a raccoon and a weasel, while Minnie and friends try to get back through the snow.

The special is produced by Wild Canary with Disney Jr., directed by Shane Prigmore, with original songs by Keith Harrison Dworkin and a new version of Jingle Bells. It is a feature-length animated film for families, in the style of the character's recent series.

A birthday screening at home

For a birthday viewing, the natural order is to start with the 1928 short, which is on Disney+ and lasts just a few minutes, and finish with the new special on the 27th. Almost a century separates them, and the theme is the same: Mickey getting out of a mess.

What we still don't know

Disney has not released the special's exact running time or its Brazilian dub cast. The release time on Disney+ has not been announced either, but the service's releases usually appear in the early hours of the scheduled date. Dates may vary by country.
