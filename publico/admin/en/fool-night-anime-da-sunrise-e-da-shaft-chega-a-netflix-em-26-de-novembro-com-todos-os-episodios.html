Fool Night, the Sunrise and Shaft anime, arrives on Netflix November 26 with every episode at once

In a world without sunlight, people agree to become plants for money to produce oxygen. The adaptation of Kasumi Yasuda's manga is the two studios' first collaboration.

By Aloha TeamNovember 14, 2026, 14:48

ShareWhatsAppXFacebookTelegram

One of the year's most unusual anime has a date. Fool Night premieres on Netflix on November 26, worldwide, with every episode at once rather than one a week, according to Anime News Network. If you like dark science fiction, it is worth marking on your calendar.

A world without sunlight

The series adapts Kasumi Yasuda's manga, published in Japan since November 2020 in Shogakukan's Big Comic Superior magazine, according to French site Animotaku. The story takes place in a future where the sky has been covered by thick clouds for about a century. Without sun, plants die and oxygen grows scarce.

Society's way out is disturbing: people can undergo a process that turns them into oxygen-producing plants, in exchange for a large sum of money. Brazilian site Critical Hits sums it up as a mix of beauty and body horror that asks how far society is willing to go to survive.

At the center of the plot is Toshiro Kamiya, a poor young man struggling to pay the bills and his sick mother's medicine. Through him, the series explores the price of that trade.

Two studios together for the first time According to the site, the partnership stands out precisely because of the contrast: carefully built science fiction worlds on one side, and on the other a visual style full of bold framing and striking colors. The trailer offers a taste of that mix.

The big draw is behind the scenes. Fool Night is the first collaboration between Sunrise and Shaft, as Anime News Network reported when Netflix announced the series at the Annecy animation festival in France in June. Sunrise, now part of Bandai Namco Filmworks, is known for science fiction such as Gundam and Code Geass; Shaft for the Monogatari series, Critical Hits notes.

It is directed by Atsushi Yukawa, with series composition by Jin Tanaka, character design by Robert Sato and music by Tatsuya Kato, according to Animotaku. The opening theme is "Shake! spit out" by the band Tempalay, according to Portuguese site OtakuPT.

Who voices the characters

In the Japanese cast, Kōki Uchiyama plays Toshiro Kamiya and Minako Kotobuki plays Yomiko Horai. In August, Anime News Network revealed three more names: Junya Enoki (Akira Yatsuka), Aya Endō (Saeko Kanaeno) and Kiyomitsu Mizuuchi (Jin Hiragishi). Netflix has also released an English dub trailer, according to What's on Netflix.

The number of episodes and their length have not been announced. Since the whole season drops at once, you can binge it the weekend after release. The subject is heavy and the tone adult, so check the age rating in the app before watching with children.

Sources: Anime News Network, Anime News Network, anúncio, Critical Hits, Animotaku, OtakuPT and What's on Netflix.
