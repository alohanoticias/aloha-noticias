I Play Rocky opens today in Brazil: the film about how Stallone fought for the role that changed his life

Directed by Peter Farrelly, the film hits Brazilian theaters on Thursday, the 19th, a day before the US and in the year Rocky turns 50.

By João GabrielNovember 19, 2026, 09:51

I Play Rocky, second trailer (ingresso.com channel, Brazilian Portuguese)
ShareWhatsAppXFacebookTelegram

I Play Rocky opens today, Thursday, November 19, in Brazilian theaters, distributed by Diamond Films. In the United States, Amazon MGM Studios releases the film tomorrow, the 20th, timed to the original Rocky's 50th anniversary. Early screenings already took place in Brazil last Saturday, the 14th, in selected theaters.

The film is not a sequel or a spin-off. It is the behind-the-scenes story: how an unknown actor wrote a script, refused to sell it unless he played the lead and ended up creating one of cinema's most famous characters.

The story

The film is set in the 1970s, when Sylvester Stallone was trying to break into Hollywood with no money and no roles. Partial facial paralysis and slurred speech kept producers away. He writes the Rocky script, a major studio wants to buy it, and he digs in: he will only sell if he stars. He turns down a large offer and accepts a tiny fee to make the film his way.

Stallone is played by Anthony Ippolito, who previously played Al Pacino in the series The Offer. Peter Farrelly, of Green Book, directs from a script by Peter Gamble.

Who's who in the cast

The film recreates the real people behind Rocky. Stephan James plays Carl Weathers, Apollo Creed; Kiki Seto plays Talia Shire, Adrian; and Robert Morgan plays Burgess Meredith, the trainer Mickey. Jay Duplass is director John G. Avildsen, while P.J. Byrne and Toby Kebbell play producers Irwin Winkler and Robert Chartoff.

Off set, Matt Dillon plays Frank Stallone Sr., the actor's father, and AnnaSophia Robb plays Sasha Czack, Stallone's first wife.

A detail in the Brazilian dub

In the Brazilian version, young Stallone is voiced by Raphael Rossatto. And Luiz Feier Motta, who spent decades dubbing Sylvester Stallone in Brazil, voices the actor's father. According to Omelete, Motta explained that the project shows a younger Stallone and so called for a younger voice.

How it was received

I Play Rocky had its world premiere on September 12 at the Toronto International Film Festival, where it was met with long applause. Critics from outlets such as Variety and Collider mostly praised Ippolito's work. There were dissenting voices too, who found the film too conventional, as often happens with biopics.

The 1976 film

Rocky opened in limited release in the US on November 13, 1976, and went nationwide on the 21st. It won three Oscars, including Best Picture and Best Director, and launched Stallone's career; he returned to the character in seven more films, counting the two Creed movies.

What about the statue?

The bronze Rocky statue was commissioned by Stallone from sculptor A. Thomas Schomberg for Rocky III (1982) and donated to the city of Philadelphia. It changed address for decades and, since 2006, had stood at the foot of the museum steps. This year the original spent a season inside the museum for an exhibition, and in September work began on its permanent installation at the top of the steps, where Rocky ends his famous run.
