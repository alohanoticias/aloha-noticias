Pokémon GO: Pawniard Community Day is today; times, bonuses and how to get Kingambit with an exclusive attack

From 2 to 5 p.m. local time, Pawniard appears more often, Eggs hatch at a quarter of the distance and evolved Bisharp becomes Kingambit with Kowtow Cleave.

By Aloha TeamNovember 21, 2026, 09:27

ShareWhatsAppXFacebookTelegram

Pokémon GO's November Community Day is today, Saturday, November 21, from 2 to 5 p.m. local time. The star is Pawniard, the Dark- and Steel-type blade Pokémon. Every city goes by its own clock, so players in different time zones simply play at 2 p.m. where they are.

By Serebii's count, it is the 107th Community Day in the game's history; the monthly events began in 2018.

What changes for three hours

During the event, Pawniard appears much more often in the wild, and the chance of finding a Shiny one goes up. According to the official announcement, some may also carry this year's Special Background, a feature Community Days gained in January 2026.

The bonuses from 2 to 5 p.m. are: Eggs hatch at one quarter of the usual distance, double Candy for catches and double the chance of Candy XL for Trainers level 31 and up. Incense activated during this window lasts three hours, and snapping a few AR photos brings a surprise.

Other bonuses run until 9 p.m.: Lure Modules last one hour and very often attract Pawniard, you can make one extra Special Trade (up to two that day) and trades cost half the Stardust.

Kingambit and the exclusive attack

The most sought-after part of the day is the final evolution. Anyone who evolves Bisharp during the event or up to four hours afterward, that is, until 9 p.m. local time, gets a Kingambit that knows the Charged Attack Kowtow Cleave. The move has 75 power in Trainer Battles and 85 in Gyms and raids, according to Niantic.

Kingambit is normally one of the game's more tedious evolutions. This Saturday the requirement is simpler: defeat one Dark- or Steel-type Raid Boss with Bisharp as your Buddy. Niantic corrected its text on October 28 to make clear the rule applies only to a Buddy Bisharp. At 9 p.m., the usual requirements return.

To get there you need Candy: Pawniard evolves into Bisharp and only then into Kingambit, so it pays to keep the catches from early in the event.

Paid research and store box

As usual, there is a paid Special Research for US$1.99 or the local equivalent in the in-game shop. It includes three Pawniard encounters with a Special Background, more Pawniard encounters, a Premium Battle Pass and a Rare Candy XL, among other items. The ticket can be gifted to Great Friends or higher, and it is nonrefundable.

On the Pokémon GO Web Store, the Community Day Ultra Ticket Box, also US$1.99, bundles the ticket with five Ultra Balls. Players who don't want to spend anything still get themed Field Research, with rewards such as Stardust, Ultra Balls and extra Pawniard encounters.

Playing in a group

Community Ambassadors, players with official support to host meetups, will run events with extra Timed Research that includes more Special Background Pawniard. Meetups near you appear on the event map on the official site. The photo above shows one such meetup in Goiânia, Brazil, in 2023.

Before you head out

Bring a power bank, have Incubators ready for the shorter distance and check whether nearby Gyms have Dark or Steel bosses to secure the evolution. And the game's usual reminder: watch traffic and your path while hunting. Niantic notes that event details may change.
