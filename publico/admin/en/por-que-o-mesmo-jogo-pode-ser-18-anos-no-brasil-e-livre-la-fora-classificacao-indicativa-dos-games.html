Why the same game can be rated 18 in Brazil and "Everyone" elsewhere: how Brazil's age ratings for games work

EA Sports FC 26 is rated E in the US and 18 in Brazil. The gap comes down to loot boxes, a new child-protection law and a criterion Brazil adopted before Europe did.

By Aloha TeamNovember 25, 2026, 13:39

Brazil's government creates a new age rating band for under-6s (in Portuguese)
ShareWhatsAppXFacebookTelegram

Pick up a copy of EA Sports FC 26 in three countries and you will find three different ages on the box. In the United States, the ESRB rated it "E" for Everyone, with a notice about in-game purchases that include random items. In Europe it launched with PEGI 3. In Brazil, since April 2026, it carries "not recommended for under-18s". It is the same game in all three places. What changes is what each system chooses to count.

Who sets the age in Brazil?

Age ratings in Brazil are handled by the Ministry of Justice and Public Security. The bands run from "general audiences" to 18, with stops at 10, 12, 14 and 16, and in October 2025 a new one was added: not recommended for under-6s, created by Ordinance No. 1,048.

Games sold only digitally do not need to request a prior review from the ministry, as long as they have self-rated through the international IARC system used by digital stores. That is why the age shows up in the store on launch day. The rating is provisional, and the government can revise it at any time.

What counts toward the rating

For years, the assessment looked at three themes: violence, sex and nudity, and drugs. The 2025 ordinance added a fourth: digital interactivity. It covers contact with unknown adults in online matches, unauthorized purchases, collection of personal data and conversations with AI agents.

In practice, a game can have no blood at all and still move up a band, purely because of what happens around the match.

What changed in 2026

The push came from the ECA Digital, Brazil's Law No. 15,211/2025 on children's rights online, which took effect on March 17, 2026. It bans reward boxes, or loot boxes, for children and teenagers. In its first month, the ministry revised the ratings of several popular games:

Moved to 18 were NBA 2K26, WWE 2K26 and EA Sports FC 26, because of loot boxes.

Moved to 16 were Roblox, Fortnite and Free Fire, over continuous-engagement mechanics and algorithmic recommendations. And to 14, MLB The Show 25 and 26, over in-game purchases. Minecraft, previously rated for general audiences, also went to 14.

Brazil's Secretary of Digital Rights, Victor Oliveira Fernandes, explained that turning on parental controls does not lower the rating: the restriction comes from the law, and the rating simply reflects it.

Doesn't anyone else look at this?

They do, and more and more. The ESRB in the United States prefers to warn: the age stays low, and a label says the game sells random items. Europe has taken Brazil's route. Since June 2026, PEGI gives 16 by default to new games with paid random items, and 18 in some cases. Because the rule applies to titles rated from that point on, the existing FC 26 box still says PEGI 3, but the next entry in the series is likely to land much higher.

What it means for players in Brazil

The age is a recommendation for families. Parents or guardians still decide what a child plays. What carries legal weight is something else: loot boxes for minors are banned by the ECA Digital, and companies selling games with that system in Brazil have to comply.

Read the descriptors too. Next to the number, the rating explains why. "Online purchases" and "interaction with strangers" matter as much as "violence" when choosing a game for a child.

The rating can change after launch. Since digital games rate themselves and the ministry reviews them, a game you bought as "general audiences" can show a different age months later. That is exactly what happened to Minecraft.

Finally, the gap between countries is not a mistake. When a game says 18 in Brazil and 3 abroad, it is usually not because it is more violent there. It is because Brazil decided to treat the in-game store as part of the content.
