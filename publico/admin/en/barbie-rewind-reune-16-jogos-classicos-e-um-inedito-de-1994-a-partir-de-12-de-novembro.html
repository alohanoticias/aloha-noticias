Barbie Rewind collects 16 classic games and an unreleased 1994 title; the collection launches November 12

Atari and Digital Eclipse gathered the doll's games released between 1991 and 2007, one that never reached stores, and a new mode for decorating the Dreamhouse.

By Aloha TeamNovember 12, 2026, 10:18

ShareWhatsAppXFacebookTelegram

If you played video games in the 1990s and 2000s, you probably ran into a Barbie game on Game Boy, Super Nintendo or PlayStation. Atari is betting on that memory: Barbie Rewind arrives on Thursday, November 12, with 16 old Barbie games in a single package.

The collection is made by Digital Eclipse, the studio known for reissuing classics with museum-like care, in partnership with Mattel. According to Video Games Chronicle, the titles originally came out between 1991 and 2007, on consoles ranging from the NES to the Game Boy Advance.

A game that never came out

The highlight for collectors is Barbie: Vacation Adventure. It was made for Super Nintendo and Mega Drive but never reached stores. Its title screen carries a 1994 date, and German site GamePro notes it sat on the shelf for more than 30 years. Barbie Rewind is the first time it can be played officially.

The other 15 range from platformers to racing and sports games. Titles already revealed include Barbie (NES, 1991), Barbie: Game Girl (Game Boy, 1992), Barbie: Race & Ride and Barbie Super Sports (PlayStation, 1999), Secret Agent Barbie: Royal Jewel Mission (Game Boy Advance, 2002) and Barbie Horse Adventures: Blue Ribbon Race (Game Boy Advance, 2003). For anyone who never played any of them, the collection works as a quick tour through almost two decades of licensed games, with visual styles that change a lot from one console to the next.

The Dreamhouse ties it together

The collection is more than a list of games. It adds a new mode for decorating Barbie's Dreamhouse. Completing tasks in the old games unlocks furniture and objects for the rooms. Croatian site Virus.hr counts more than 250 pieces, inspired by real Barbie toys from over 65 years, and three house styles to choose from.

Tasks are given by characters from the line, such as Barbie, Ken, Teresa and Christie, and also reward experience points and content about the doll's history. It is a way to give players a reason to return to each game instead of trying them once and moving on.

Platforms and price

Barbie Rewind comes to PS5, PS4, Xbox Series X|S, Xbox One, Switch, Switch 2 and PC. The digital version costs US$29.99. There is a physical edition for PS5 and Switch, plus a Switch Deluxe Edition at US$59.99 that includes a Barbie doll wearing an Atari T-shirt. Regional prices were not in the sources we checked, so look at your console's store on launch day.

The date stands out too: the launch is one week before GTA 6, scheduled for November 19. Spanish site Vandal joked that Barbie is not afraid of Rockstar. For anyone who wants something lighter mid-month, it is a very different option, and the trailer above shows how the games and the decorating mode look in practice.

Sources: Video Games Chronicle, Virus.hr, GamePro, Nintenderos, Vandal and Steam.
