Dune: Part Three opens December 17 in Brazil: what to remember before the trilogy ends

Denis Villeneuve's final film adapts Dune Messiah, has early screenings on December 15 and 16 in Brazil and opens the same day as the new Avengers.

By Aloha TeamNovember 17, 2026, 10:12

ShareWhatsAppXFacebookTelegram

One month to go. Dune: Part Three opens in Brazilian theaters on December 17, according to Warner Bros. Pictures Brasil's own dubbed final trailer, and the studio has scheduled early screenings on December 15 and 16 in selected theaters. Advance tickets are already on sale. Dates in other countries differ.

This is the end of Denis Villeneuve's trilogy. After splitting Frank Herbert's first novel into two films, the director now adapts Dune Messiah, the 1969 sequel that shows what happens to Paul Atreides once he takes power.

What is official

The Brazilian date is December 17; Warner says the international rollout starts on December 16, and the US release is on the 18th. The film was shot for IMAX, as the campaign keeps repeating. Timothée Chalamet and Zendaya return as Paul and Chani, and the crew is the same as on the first two: Linus Sandgren on cinematography, Patrice Vermette on production design, Joe Walker on editing and Hans Zimmer on the score.

What to remember from the first two films

In Dune (2021), House Atreides is handed control of Arrakis, the desert planet that produces the spice, and falls into a Harkonnen trap backed by the emperor. Paul and his mother, Jessica, survive and take refuge with the Fremen, the desert people.

In Dune: Part Two (2024), Paul wins the Fremen's trust, grows close to Chani, defeats Feyd-Rautha in a duel and forces the emperor to give up the throne. The film ends with the holy war he feared about to begin, and with Chani walking away, unwilling to accept the path Paul chose.

Dune Messiah picks up from there. Herbert wrote the second book to show the dangerous side of the charismatic hero, and that bitter tone is what the final trailer suggests: less desert combat, more palace intrigue and the fallout of everything Paul has done.

Dune and the Avengers on the same day

In Brazil, December 17 is also the release date of Avengers: Doomsday. Two big films on the same day mean a fight for IMAX screens and the best showtimes, especially over the first weekends. Anyone set on seeing Dune on the giant screen has two options: buy early, or catch the December 15 and 16 early screenings, which usually face less competition.

Is a rewatch worth it?

It helps, but it takes time. Dune runs 2h35 and Part Two runs 2h46, more than five hours and twenty minutes in total. If you can't, the essentials fit in one sentence: Paul is emperor, the Fremen see him as a messiah, and the war he foresaw is beginning. The film itself should cover the rest.

What we still don't know

Warner has not yet released Part Three's running time or its Brazilian age rating. There is also no date for streaming: the first two films respected their theatrical window before arriving at home, and that will most likely happen again.
