From 99 Nights in the Forest to Grow a Garden: the Roblox games already heading to the big screen

Hollywood studios have bought the rights to games made inside Roblox. What is confirmed, who is attached and what has not been said.

By João GabrielNovember 20, 2026, 13:22

Roblox game adaptations for the movies (video in English)
ShareWhatsAppXFacebookTelegram

Films based on video games are nothing new, but games built inside Roblox have become a fresh source of projects. At least three already have adaptations in development, according to the US trade press. None has a release date. Here is what has been confirmed so far, in the order the news appeared.

November 2025: Grow a Garden

Last November, Deadline reported that the production company Story Kitchen had struck a deal with the New Zealand studio Splitting Point to bring Grow a Garden to the cinema. The plant-growing game launched in March 2025 and at one point had 22.3 million concurrent players, according to Story Kitchen. The planned story follows city kids who discover a portal into the game's world, go on a journey and return to the real one. “If we do our job right, this will be a family story and gets people to go outside,” said Dmitri Johnson, the company's co-founder. There is no director, cast or date.

October 2025: Jailbreak

Around the same time, Vancouver's Wind Sun Sky Entertainment agreed to adapt Jailbreak, the cops-and-robbers game made by Badimo. CEO Catherine Winder said the film will use the game's mechanics as a loose suggestion for the plot and mix “action, comedy and heart-driven storytelling”, according to Story Kitchen, which reproduces the announcement. No date there either.

April 2026: 99 Nights in the Forest

The most recent case is the survival-horror game from Grandma's Favourite Games, launched in March 2025. On April 13, TheWrap reported that 20th Century Studios acquired the theatrical rights in a “highly competitive bid”, with J.R. Young overseeing the project for the studio. Players must survive 99 nights in a haunted forest, rescue four lost children and escape creatures such as the deer. According to TheWrap, it is the seventh most played game in Roblox history, with nearly 26 billion visits and a peak of 14.2 million players. GosuGamers cites 14.1 million, a small difference worth recording.

The studio's three creators, Alec Kieft, Matthew Hufton and Cameron Angland, are executive producers. David Greenbaum, president of Disney live action and 20th Century Studios, called them “exactly the kind of bold, imaginative creators we love working with”. The team says it wants to explore the game's world “on a broader stage”.

Who is making the film

In the first report, no director or writer was set. Later, GosuGamers, citing The Hollywood Reporter, said that Sam Raimi (of the Evil Dead trilogy, the Tobey Maguire Spider-Man films and Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness) and Josh Cooley (of Toy Story 4 and Transformers One) had taken on the project. That information appears only in that source among those I read, and the text does not detail each person's exact role.

What is still unknown

In the articles I read, none of the three films has a release date, cast or settled synopsis beyond what is cited above. GosuGamers also mentions that Steal a Brainrot, another hit on the platform, is reportedly in development too, without further detail. Story Kitchen notes that Roblox, which provides tools to creators and does not develop games itself, did not respond about its role in the projects. For now, these are deals and promises. Whether any of them reaches screens is a question for the coming years.
