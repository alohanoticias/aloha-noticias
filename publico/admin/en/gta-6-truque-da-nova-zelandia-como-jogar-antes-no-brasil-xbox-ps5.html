GTA 6 New Zealand trick: how to play early on Xbox and PS5

New Zealand gets GTA 6 16 hours before Brazil because of the time zone. Step by step on Xbox and PS5, what it costs and what can go wrong.

By João GabrielNovember 11, 2026, 19:39

ShareWhatsAppXFacebookTelegram

The story is true and the reason is simple: GTA 6 unlocks at midnight on November 19 in each region's local time, and New Zealand is the first major market to reach that midnight. So New Zealand players start 16 hours before people in Brazil, the site's home base. Along with that, the so-called “New Zealand trick” is making the rounds again, promising anyone an earlier start. Here is how to do it on Xbox and PS5, what it costs and what can go wrong.

In short: on Xbox, the route is changing the console's location to New Zealand. It works if the console follows the New Zealand unlock, which nobody has guaranteed. On PS5, changing the clock does nothing: you need a New Zealand account and a second copy of the game. Rockstar, Microsoft and Sony have not commented on the method.

Why New Zealand plays first

The New Zealand store lists the unlock at 11:00 UTC on November 18, which is midnight on the 19th in New Zealand summer time. In Brasília time that is 8 a.m. on Wednesday, November 18. The Brazilian PS5 store lists 3:00 UTC on the 19th, which is midnight in Brasília: a 16-hour gap. The rollout ends in Mexico and Central America at 6:00 UTC on the 19th, about 19 hours after New Zealand.

How to play early on Xbox: step by step

The method has been around for years and was used on launches such as Call of Duty and Forza Horizon. The idea is to make the console “think” it is in New Zealand. These are the steps shared by gaming sites:

1. Have GTA 6 fully downloaded through the pre-load, on your usual account.

2. On the Xbox, open Settings and go to System.

3. Open Language & location and change Location to New Zealand.

4. Restart the console.

5. From 8 a.m. on Wednesday (Nov 18), Brasília time, try launching the game. If the Xbox follows the New Zealand unlock, it opens.

6. Whenever you want, set Location back to your country the same way.

Windows Central, which explains the steps, also notes that Rockstar and Take-Two may have some way to stop the game from opening a day early with this change. So far there is no confirmation that it will work with GTA 6.

How to play early on PS5: step by step

On PlayStation the store region is tied to the account, not the console. Changing the PS5's time zone or clock does not turn a Brazilian account into a New Zealand one. The route described by gaming sites requires a second purchase:

1. Create a new PlayStation account with New Zealand as the country.

2. With that account, buy GTA 6 from the New Zealand store. The standard edition costs NZ$139.95, and the payment has to be accepted by that country's store.

3. On your PS5, turn on Console Sharing for that account. That lets you play on your usual account.

4. Download the game and wait for 8 a.m. on Wednesday (Nov 18), Brasília time.

The risks before you try

First, none of this is official. Rockstar has only confirmed the pre-load and launch dates, and Microsoft and Sony have said nothing about the trick. Second, Microsoft's support page warns that things bought in one region may not work in another and recommends changing region only when you actually move. Third, there are no reports of bans for changing region, but no guarantee that it is safe either. And be wary of anyone selling “early access” to GTA 6: scams like that are common around big launches. On PS5 there is one more catch: the PlayStation Network terms of service ask you to pick your country of residence when you create an account. Opening a New Zealand account while living in Brazil goes against Sony's rules.

Is it worth it?

In my view, for Xbox players the attempt is cheap: a few minutes in the settings, and you can switch back to your real region afterwards. On PS5, paying for a second copy to gain 16 hours, while going against Sony's terms, makes little sense for most people. There is also a side effect for everyone: from 8 a.m. Brasília time on Wednesday people will already be playing, so if you want to avoid spoilers, mute words like “GTA 6” on social media until your own unlock. If your copy is not downloaded yet, see how to keep the pre-load running.
