Disney's Hexed sends a teenager to a hidden town of witches; the animated film opens in Brazil on November 26

Walt Disney Animation Studios' 65th feature stars Hailee Steinfeld as the lead and has a look that recalls the studio's hand-drawn classics.

By Aloha TeamNovember 10, 2026, 14:37

ShareWhatsAppXFacebookTelegram

Billie has always felt out of place. At school, at home, in the suburban neighborhood where she lives with her perfectionist mother. In Hexed, Disney's new original animated film (titled *Enfeitiçadas: Bem-vindos a Hexe* in Brazil), she finds out that feeling has an explanation: magical powers that had been dormant and wake up without warning. The film opens in Brazilian theaters on November 26, in cinemas only.

A teenager, a mother and a hidden town

According to the synopsis released by the studio, Billie is impulsive and unconventional. When her powers appear, she is pulled out of ordinary life and into Hexe, a magical realm of witches, where she is welcomed by two unusual guides: Ms. Quill, an enchanted quill, and Elias Quire, a magical diary. Along the way, Billie uncovers family secrets that could change the world of witches forever. Her mother is not left out: according to Ingresso.com, the discovery turns both their lives upside down and drags them into that secret world of spells.

The trailer shown at CinemaCon in April gives a sense of the tone. As Ingresso.com describes it, Billie falls into a dark lair overgrown with twisted vines, where a talking book and quill quiz her to find out whether she is really a witch. She passes, and a portal opens onto a colorful, Renaissance-inspired village surrounded by hills.

Who is behind the film

It is Walt Disney Animation Studios' 65th animated feature. The latest credits list Fawn Veerasunthorn and Jason Hand, co-director of *Moana 2*, as directors, with Josie Trinidad as co-director. When the project was announced in August 2025, the directing pair named was Trinidad and Hand, so the team changed along the way. Roy Conli, of *Big Hero 6*, produces.

The directors keep coming back to one idea: the film is about fitting in. "Hexe is a place where Billie begins to feel seen for the first time in her life," Hand said when the trailer came out. Veerasunthorn described Billie as someone who has felt miscast in her own life and only finds herself after leaving the normal world behind.

The voices

In the original version, Hailee Steinfeld voices Billie and Rashida Jones plays Alice, her cautious mother. Tracey Ullman voices Ms. Quill and Stephen Fry voices Elias Quire. In the Brazilian dub, actress Letícia Spiller voices Queen Celeste, according to Legião dos Heróis. The rest of the Brazilian cast had not appeared in the sources we checked.

A look unlike recent films

The first images, shown at Destination D23 in Orlando, drew attention for their style. AdoroCinema noted that the colorful, hand-drawn feel recalls the studio's older films more than the 3D of *Moana 2* or *Wish*. The first official artwork showed Hexe itself: a village full of creatures and crooked buildings.

Release dates around the world

Sources differ slightly here. Ingresso.com and Legião dos Heróis give November 26 for Brazil, while Diário do Grande ABC published November 25, which is the U.S. date. In the UK, it opens on the 27th; dates in other countries may differ. Check your local cinema listings before buying a ticket. The release is theatrical only, and none of the sources mention a date for streaming.

Runtime and age rating are not in the sources we checked. We also have not found any published review yet. What can be said now is that Disney is betting on an original story, and the trailer makes the target audience clear: families and anyone who grew up with the studio's classics.

Sources: Ingresso.com (in Portuguese), Ingresso.com, CinemaCon (in Portuguese), Diário do Grande ABC (in Portuguese), Legião dos Heróis (in Portuguese), AdoroCinema (in Portuguese), Laughing Place, The Hollywood News and BroadwayWorld.
