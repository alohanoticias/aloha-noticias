World of Warcraft: Forever arrived on November 4: what Classic+ is and who can play

Blizzard has launched a third version of the game, based on the original Azeroth, with the level cap locked at 60 and new stories.

By João GabrielNovember 16, 2026, 18:27

World of Warcraft: Forever, gameplay trailer (official World of Warcraft channel, in English)
ShareWhatsAppXFacebookTelegram

World of Warcraft: Forever, the version fans called "Classic+", arrived on November 4, 2026. Blizzard presented the project at BlizzCon 2026 as a permanent place inside the game, based on the original Azeroth, and it now lives alongside current WoW and WoW Classic.

What it is, in a few words

According to Blizzard's official page, Forever reimagines the original Azeroth, with new stories and adventures and a focus on community. Game Over Drive describes it as a third official WoW experience that replaces neither of the other two. The journey runs from level 1 to 60, and Blizzard built a Portal Room to help players find friends and groups right from the start.

The maximum level is 60 and, according to Game Over Drive, it does not rise with each update. Growth is horizontal: new quests, gear, reputations, professions, dungeons and raids. Anyone who remembers the WoW of 2004 will recognize the structure, but with content that did not exist then.

What is new on the map

The same guide lists four new areas, using the names from the Brazilian Portuguese version: Ilha de Zefras, the starting zone for levels 1 to 12; Sendas do Rio, a river region with more than 150 new quests; Shen'dralas, in Kalimdor, tied to the Dire Maul complex; and Monte Hyjal, the central endgame area, connected to one of the new raids.

Who can play and how much it costs

Here the sources do not agree on everything, so it is worth separating them. Blizzard's official page gives the worldwide launch as November 4 at 8 p.m. Brasília time, but does not detail prices or subscription. Game Over Drive states that the full game is included with an active World of Warcraft subscription or Game Time, with no expansion purchase. The same text admits that Blizzard's own Brazilian pages list either 7 p.m. or 8 p.m. as the start time, so that detail remained ambiguous.

The site Instant Gaming, in a Portugal Portuguese edition, reported the launch on November 5. The difference is time zone: 8 p.m. in Brasília on the 4th is already the early hours of the 5th in Europe. The game is installed through the Battle.net app and has official controller support.

The bundles and the beta

For those who wanted to get in earlier, there was a closed beta from September 17, due to end on October 21, according to Game Over Drive. There are also three sale bundles. The Heroic one brings access, the starting experience on Ilha de Zefras, name reservation, a mount and cosmetics. The Epic adds beta access, 30 days of game time and more cosmetics. The Warcraft Forever Collection includes all of that, plus Warcraft III: Reforged and the Reino dos Renegados campaign. The guide does not give the prices of these bundles.

What to expect

Forever promises longevity instead of a race. With no level increases, the appeal becomes what you do afterwards: dungeons, reputations, professions and the new raids. For anyone with an active subscription, the barrier to entry is low. For those without one, the cost depends on the subscription plan, which the sources do not detail.
