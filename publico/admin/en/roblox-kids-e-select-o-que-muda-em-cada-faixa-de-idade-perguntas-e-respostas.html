Roblox Kids and Select: what changes at each age, in questions and answers

Separate accounts for under-16s, restricted chat and selfie age checks. What the company says is changing, and what is still unclear.

By Aloha TeamNovember 16, 2026, 13:23

ShareWhatsAppXFacebookTelegram

Since June, Roblox has split the accounts of anyone under 16 into two groups, Kids and Select, each with its own rules for games and chat. We gathered the most common questions with what the company itself and Olhar Digital report, and marked what is still unanswered. The numbers and rules below are the ones published in May and June and may change as the company adjusts the system.

What is the difference between Kids and Select?

Kids is for children aged 5 to 8. Chat comes switched off, and a linked parent or guardian can turn it on in games that offer the option. Only games rated Minimal or Mild that went through the expanded selection process appear, and the account gets a blue background. Select is for ages 9 to 15: Moderate-rated games are added, and chat varies by age and region, always limited to people in the same age group. Where available, children can also talk with “Trusted Friends”, people they know outside the platform. The regular account, for verified users aged 16 and over, reaches everything except Restricted (18+) games.

What stays out of these catalogs?

According to the company, games with social interaction or free-form building, those dealing with sensitive topics, Roblox Moments (clip sharing) and one-on-one chat inside games.

How does Roblox find out someone's age?

There are two routes: an official photo ID or facial age estimation through the device's camera. The company says images and video are deleted after processing and that the technology was independently tested, with a mean error of up to 1.4 years for under-18s. That figure comes only from Roblox, so treat it as the company's claim. Chat requires verification: without it, nobody chats at any age. Olhar Digital describes the same scheme, with the selfie unlocking features and documents requested if the user disputes the result.

What if the estimate is wrong?

Anyone estimated at 18 or older can reset their age once in settings. Parents with a linked account can also correct their child's age once after the initial check, through Parental Controls, and a valid ID settles the matter. The company adds that it monitors behavior signals and may ask for a new check if the apparent age does not match the profile.

What can parents control?

With a linked account, parents can adjust chat, block or allow games and friends, set time and spending limits, check or update the date of birth and see activity: games played, time spent and the friends list. According to Olhar Digital, these controls were more limited for younger users and now reach users up to 15.

Does this already apply in Brazil?

The sources do not add up here. The company's post, dated May 20, says Kids and Select began in Australia, Indonesia, the Netherlands and New Zealand, with a global launch in early June, and does not mention Brazil. Olhar Digital, on June 16, puts Brazil in the headline but describes the system as global, in force since that Tuesday. During the transition, the age the user declared still sets the catalog (5 to 8, Kids; 9 and up, Select), and chat still depends on verification. Families with children playing should check in the app what appears on their accounts.

Sources: Roblox (in Portuguese), Olhar Digital (in Portuguese).
