Magic Knight Rayearth: the remake is airing; what changed, who is making it and where to watch

The new take on CLAMP's work premiered on October 7, streams on Wednesdays on Crunchyroll and runs two consecutive cours, with the finale expected in March 2027.

By Aloha TeamNovember 16, 2026, 18:27

Magic Knight Rayearth, official trailer with Portuguese subtitles (Crunchyroll Brasil channel)
ShareWhatsAppXFacebookTelegram

Anyone who grew up with Magic Knight Rayearth (aired in Brazil on SBT as Guerreiras Mágicas de Rayearth) can now get a new episode every week. The remake of CLAMP's work premiered in Japan on October 7 and arrives the same day on Crunchyroll, which streams the series in South America.

In Japan, the anime airs on Wednesdays at 11:45 p.m. in TV Asahi's IMAnimation W block. In Brazil, episodes land on the platform a few hours later, with Portuguese subtitles.

A remake, not a sequel

The new series restarts the story from scratch. Hikaru Shidou, Umi Ryuuzaki and Fuu Hououji are three students who don't know each other and, during a school trip to Tokyo Tower, are taken to the world of Cephiro by Princess Emeraude. To save her, they must become the legendary Magic Knights.

So anyone who never saw the old anime can start with the 2026 version. Crunchyroll itself treats the series as a reboot, and the project was announced in July 2024, for the original anime's 30th anniversary.

Who is making it

Animation is by studio E&H production, with planning by TMS Entertainment. Yui Miura directs, in his series directing debut. Shigeru Murakoshi handles series composition, Satomi Watanabe the character designs, and Yuki Kajiura scores it with Takumi Ozawa and Shiho Terada.

The Japanese voices are new too: Ayane Sakura plays Hikaru, Rumi Okubo is Umi and Rie Takahashi is Fuu. The cast also includes Saori Hayami as Emeraude, Yuki Ono as Zagato, Mika Kikuchi as Mokona and Yoshitsugu Matsuoka as Ferio.

The opening is Otome no Ken, sung by Toaka, and the ending is Seira, by Yurina Kawaguchi.

From announcement to premiere

The cast and first trailer were presented on stage at AnimeJapan on March 28, 2026. The first episode had its world premiere at Anime Expo in the United States on July 4, attended by Rie Takahashi and Saori Hayami. In September, a new trailer revealed the opening and ending songs and more cast members.

How many episodes

The series was announced as two consecutive cours, roughly six months on air. According to Omelete, the finale is expected in March 2027. The exact episode count has not been announced.

For comparison: the 1994 anime had 49 episodes, split into two arcs of 20 and 29, and aired in Japan until 1995. Three OVAs with an alternate version of the story followed in 1997.

What about a dub?

So far, no Portuguese dub has been announced for the remake. The series was not on Crunchyroll's initial dub list for the October season. If a Brazilian version comes, it's not yet known whether the classic cast will return.

Where to watch the original

The classic anime is available on Netflix and Crunchyroll in Brazil. The official TMS Anime Latino YouTube channel also has episodes dubbed in Portuguese and the three 1997 OVAs. CLAMP's manga began publication in 1993.

CLAMP, the group of creators behind the work, also made Cardcaptor Sakura, Chobits and xxxHOLiC. In Brazil, Rayearth reached free-to-air TV in 1996 on SBT, and that is where many people first met Hikaru, Umi, Fuu and little Mokona.
