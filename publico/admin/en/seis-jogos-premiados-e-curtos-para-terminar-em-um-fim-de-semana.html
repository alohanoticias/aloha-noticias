Six short, award-winning games you can finish in a weekend

Not every good game asks for 80 hours. This list gathers acclaimed titles that fit into a Saturday and a Sunday, with each one's average length and where to play it.

By João GabrielNovember 28, 2026, 18:52

Games to finish in a weekend (Cogumelando, in Portuguese)
ShareWhatsAppXFacebookTelegram

A pile of games started and never finished is a reality for many people, and it isn't for lack of will. Many big releases go past 50 hours, and fitting that into a week of work or study isn't simple. The good news is that some of the most acclaimed games of the past decade fit into a weekend. The six below were picked for that reason, and the time listed is the main-story average on HowLongToBeat, a site that gathers playtimes reported by players themselves.

A Short Hike (about 1.5 hours)

It's the shortest on the list and perhaps the lightest. You play Claire, who spends a few days in a forest park and needs to reach the top of a mountain to get a phone signal. Along the way, you can glide, fish, help other visitors and get lost on trails that lead nowhere. The game was made almost entirely by one person, Adam Robinson-Yu, and it's on PC, Switch, PlayStation 4 and Xbox One.

What Remains of Edith Finch (about 2 hours)

Edith returns to the family home, a strange building full of sealed rooms, to understand why almost all the Finches died young. Each room tells the story of a relative, and each story plays differently. It won the BAFTA for best game in 2018. Available on PC, PlayStation, Xbox and Switch, it's ideal for a single session, preferably at night.

GRIS (about 3.5 hours)

Made by the Spanish studio Nomada, GRIS is a platformer without dialogue or death, in which the heroine restores color to a ruined world as she moves forward. The watercolor-style art is the main reason to play, and the soundtrack follows every change of scenery. It's on PC, current consoles and mobile.

INSIDE (about 4 hours)

From the same studio as Limbo, Denmark's Playdead, INSIDE follows a boy fleeing through a farm, a town and a laboratory that grow darker and darker. There isn't a single word in the whole game. The puzzles are short and fit the pacing well, and the ending is one of the most discussed of the decade. Available on PC, PlayStation, Xbox, Switch and iPhone.

Firewatch (about 4 hours)

In 1989, Henry takes a job as a fire lookout in a tower in Wyoming's Shoshone forest. His only human contact is Delilah, his supervisor, with whom he talks by radio. The game is almost all conversation and walking, and the relationship between the two carries the story. It's on PC, PlayStation 4, Xbox One and Switch.

Portal 2 (about 8.5 hours)

The longest on the list still fits in a weekend, with a little discipline. You solve tests with a gun that creates portals on walls and floors, guided by an artificial intelligence with a questionable sense of humor. The co-op campaign, separate from the main one, is great to play as a pair afterward. It came out in 2011 for PC and the consoles of the time and arrived on Switch in 2022, in the Portal: Companion Collection.

How to choose

If you want something relaxing, A Short Hike and GRIS are the safest bets. For those who like stories, Edith Finch and Firewatch are the best options. INSIDE suits anyone who doesn't mind a heavy mood, and Portal 2 offers the most brain-teasing challenge. All six go on sale often in digital stores, so it's worth adding them to your wishlist and waiting for a discount.
