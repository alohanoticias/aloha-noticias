Fast & Furious returns to Brazilian cinemas on November 12 for the first film's 25th anniversary

Universal is bringing back the 2001 original in a special re-release. What is official, what the announcements leave out, and the popcorn buckets that may not make it here.

By João GabrielNovember 15, 2026, 17:22

Velozes e Furiosos | Re-release | Official trailer, subtitled (Ingresso.com channel)
ShareWhatsAppXFacebookTelegram

The Fast and the Furious (2001), released in Brazil as Velozes e Furiosos, returns to Brazilian screens on November 12, a Thursday, in a special re-release from Universal Pictures marking 25 years since the film that started the franchise. The date appears in the announcement relayed by Ingresso.com and in pieces from Omelete and BNews, all dated October 15. Release dates in other countries may differ.

What is already official

The film is from 2001, directed by Rob Cohen, with Vin Diesel (Dominic Toretto), Paul Walker (Brian O'Conner), Michelle Rodriguez, Jordana Brewster and Rick Yune in the cast. Ingresso.com cites a screenplay by David Ayer and Gary Scott Thompson and a worldwide box office of about US$212 million. Universal calls the comeback a "special re-release," and the commemorative re-release trailer, with subtitles, is already out: it is the video in this article, posted on the Ingresso.com channel.

As for the age rating, only BNews reports it and says "Livre" (all ages, in Brazil's rating system). Ingresso.com and Omelete do not mention it, and it stands out for a film about illegal street racing, so check your cinema's website before taking children.

25 years in numbers

The math from 2001 to 2026 checks out: 25 years. The other calculation is more interesting. Ingresso.com and BNews say the franchise has 11 films and tops US$7 billion at the box office. If the first film earned about US$212 million, it accounts for roughly 3% of that total (simple math: 212 divided by 7,000). In other words, the chapter that started it all is a small slice of what the series became. The next step already has a date: Fast Forever, set for March 17, 2028, according to Ingresso.com and Omelete.

What changes for moviegoers in Brazil

This is the part the announcements do not settle. Ingresso.com said on October 15 that ticket sales for the first screenings would be announced soon on its site and apps. None of the three pages we read gives a price, screen formats (IMAX, 3D and the like), the film's running time, whether screenings will be dubbed or subtitled, or whether the version shown is restored or adds anything new. The only practical certainty is the date. Other films also open that Thursday: our list of November releases can help you decide what to see.

And the popcorn merchandise?

In June, Omelete showed a Cinemark popcorn bucket shaped like Toretto's black 1970 Dodge Charger R/T and, in August, a cup shaped like a NOS cylinder. The article about the bucket says it would reach US cinemas in August and admits there is no way to know whether it comes to Brazil. The one about the cup does not name a country, and neither links the items to the Brazilian re-release. If you saw the photo and thought about rushing to the cinema, ask first.

And to watch at home?

A sponsored piece in Rolling Stone Brasil, dated September 2, announced the complete collection of all 11 films on Universal+ in Brazil. Since it is paid content from the company and a single source, open the app's catalog before counting on it.

What remains open

Ticket price, screens, dubbing and running time still need to appear. We also found no information on how many cinemas and cities will hold screenings, and that is the most practical question for anyone living outside the state capitals.
