Metroid Ravenous arrives January 28 on Switch 2: what we know about the new 2D Metroid

Samus is stranded on an unknown moon and can now devour enemies. The game is a Switch 2 exclusive and gets a special edition with an amiibo.

By João GabrielNovember 18, 2026, 09:46

Metroid Ravenous, official gameplay trailer (posted by GameSpot)
ShareWhatsAppXFacebookTelegram

Samus Aran's return to two dimensions is just over two months away. Metroid Ravenous launches on January 28, 2027, exclusively on Nintendo Switch 2. Nintendo revealed it in the September Nintendo Direct with a release date already set, which is rare for a debut announcement.

It is the first new 2D Metroid since Metroid Dread in 2021. In between, the series got Metroid Prime 4: Beyond, in 3D, released in December 2025. Ravenous returns to the side-scrolling exploration that gave rise to the term metroidvania.

What Nintendo has said

In the official description, Samus is attacked by unknown assailants and left stranded on an unfamiliar moon full of labyrinthine ruins. Players gather weapons and gear, push through corridors and face powerful bosses, as the series demands.

The twist is in the name. Ravenous means voracious: Samus's Metroid instincts awaken and she can consume enemies. Nintendo describes fast, aggressive combat with beams and missiles, and the trailer shows close-range attacks far more violent than in Dread.

Editions and amiibo

Alongside the game comes an amiibo line with two figures sold separately: Samus and a Chozo Statue. There is also a Special Edition with the game, a steel case and an exclusive amiibo of Samus in the Metroid Suit.

Nintendo has not yet explained what each amiibo unlocks in the game. In previous entries, the figures gave helper items and extras, but that is not confirmed for Ravenous.

The 2D series in brief

For newcomers: the main 2D line began with Metroid (1986), followed by Metroid II (1991), Super Metroid (1994), Metroid Fusion (2002) and Metroid: Zero Mission (2004). After a long gap came Metroid: Samus Returns (2017) and Metroid Dread (2021), both made by Spanish studio MercurySteam with Nintendo. Nintendo has not said who is developing Ravenous, so any name is speculation for now.

You do not need to have played the earlier games. Each 2D Metroid stands on its own, and Ravenous's premise, Samus lost in a new place without her gear, is exactly the series' classic entry point.

What it means for players in Brazil

The game is Switch 2 only: anyone still on the original Switch will not be able to run it. Curiously, the name surfaced first in Brazil. On July 1, two months before the announcement, the Brazilian Ministry of Justice's age-rating system listed Metroid Ravenous as not recommended for under-12s, with violence as the content descriptor, and the entry was deleted shortly after. That rating already applies to the Brazilian release. As of publication, we found no official price in Brazilian reais for the standard version or the Special Edition. Prices may differ in other countries.

What we still don't know

Still missing: download size, estimated campaign length, language list and Brazilian price. Nintendo usually releases a final trailer or a dedicated presentation in the weeks before launch, so more details should surface by January.
