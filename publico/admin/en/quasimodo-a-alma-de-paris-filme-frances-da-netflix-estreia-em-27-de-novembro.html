The Hunchback, Soul of Paris, Netflix's French film, premieres November 27 and imagines who inspired Quasimodo

Vincent Cassel plays a real man who may have served as the model for Victor Hugo's character, in an 1830 Paris on the eve of revolution.

By Aloha TeamNovember 12, 2026, 18:47

ShareWhatsAppXFacebookTelegram

The Hunchback of Notre-Dame has been a novel, a musical and a Disney cartoon. Now it becomes a period drama. The Hunchback, Soul of Paris premieres on Netflix on November 27, and its angle is different: instead of retelling the book, the film imagines the life of the man who may have inspired Victor Hugo to create the famous bell-ringer.

It is a French production directed by Jean-François Richet and starring Vincent Cassel, according to Brazilian site Omelete. The cast also includes Benjamin Voisin, Karim Leklou and Daphné Patakia. The announced runtime is about 1 hour and 43 minutes, according to Argentine newspaper El Sol.

Paris in 1830

The story takes place in Paris on the eve of the July Revolution of 1830, which toppled King Charles X. French site Echos du Net adds another element: the city lives under the threat of cholera, and a hunchbacked stonemason becomes a target of the police. Cassel plays that man.

Victor Hugo himself appears as a character, according to Dutch site Binge. That makes sense: the novel Notre-Dame de Paris, where Quasimodo comes from, was published in 1831, right after these events. Daphné Patakia plays Benedetta, whom Journal du Geek describes as an Esmeralda who does not say her own name.

Inspired, not adapted

It is worth adjusting expectations. The film follows neither the plot of the book nor the tone of the 1996 animated movie. Netflix speaks of "the man who may have inspired" the character, which makes clear this is a historical hypothesis turned into fiction, not a proven biography. The trailer, released in late October, leans on a dark mood, chases and the cathedral itself as a setting.

The film reunites Cassel and Richet, who have worked together before, as Sortir à Paris points out. Richet became known internationally for Mesrine, about French criminal Jacques Mesrine, also with Cassel in the lead role.

Different names abroad

Anyone searching for the film in other countries may be confused by the title. In English it is called The Hunchback, Soul of Paris, after going through other working titles, according to What's on Netflix. In France it is Quasimodo, l'âme de Paris. In Brazil, Netflix uses Quasímodo: A Alma de Paris.

The cathedral is also a character off screen. Notre-Dame was hit by a major fire in 2019 and reopened in December 2024 after a long restoration. Anyone who wants to see the place that inspired Hugo, and now the film, can visit it again.

The film premieres on Friday, November 27, for all subscribers, with no extra charge. Netflix has not said which dubbed versions will be available at launch; the official trailer above has Portuguese subtitles. An age rating had not been published in the sources we checked.

Sources: Omelete, Omelete, pôster, What's on Netflix, Sortir à Paris, Echos du Net, Journal du Geek, Binge and El Sol.
